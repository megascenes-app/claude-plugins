# Birthday Banner Maker

Design birthday banners with your greeting and the name in the colors of the party, to print and hang or to show on a screen.

## What it does
Give the words for the banner, the name and the colors of the party: pink and gold, blue and silver, rainbow, black and gold, with balloons, flowers, dinosaurs, outer space or a tropical theme. You get a wide or square banner design with your words in it, to print and hang on the wall or to show on the TV at the party. For parents and hosts decorating a birthday party at home, in the office or at a venue. Claude tells you the price first and makes it in your MegaScenes account when you say yes.

**How the files are made:** each birthday banner is generated by an image model. Nothing is made until you say yes, and the price in credits comes first.

Limitations: designs as image files, wide or square; very long strips are not available, so print a long banner in parts. Your text is passed to the model as written, so check the lettering in the result. It does not print or ship the banner. Making a file needs a signed-in MegaScenes account and runs in it; nothing is made until you confirm. Not for sexual or adult content or for real people's likeness. Files are made by generative models and saved in your MegaScenes library.

## Install
In Claude Code:
```
/plugin marketplace add megascenes-app/claude-plugins
/plugin install birthday-banner@megascenes
```

## Connecting
- Birthday Banner Maker talks to the MegaScenes server at `https://megascenes.com/api/claude/birthday-banner/mcp` (Streamable HTTP).
- Connecting opens MegaScenes: sign in, create an account, or continue without one and add your email when you pay (OAuth with PKCE). Then you come straight back to the chat.
- Each birthday banner uses credits from your MegaScenes account and is priced before it runs; new accounts can start with a low-cost trial week.
- The birthday banners show in a viewer in the chat where Claude supports interactive apps; in Claude Code you get the links.

## Tools
| Tool | What it does |
|---|---|
| `list_birthday_banner_models` | Models for birthday banners with settings and starting prices. Read-only. |
| `estimate_birthday_banner_cost` | Exact price of one birthday banner before it runs. Read-only. |
| `create_birthday_banner` | Starts one birthday banner in your account. Uses credits; needs sign-in. |
| `get_birthday_banner` | Status and file links. Read-only. |
| `get_account` | Your plan and credit balance. Read-only. |

## Example prompts
- "A pink and gold Happy Birthday banner with balloons"
- "A black and gold birthday banner that says Happy Birthday Marco"
- "A rainbow birthday banner with a tropical theme for the party screen"

## Data and privacy
What you give for the birthday banner (the details you give) goes to MegaScenes, stays in your MegaScenes account with the finished file, and is passed to the model provider that makes it. The plugin has no hooks or local scripts and sends nothing else.
