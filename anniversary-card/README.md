# Anniversary Card Maker

Design anniversary cards with your names, the years together, your message and your signature in the design, in the look you choose.

## What it does
Give the names, the years you are celebrating, the main line, a short message and how you sign it, and pick the look: romantic red roses, elegant gold, watercolor florals, minimal, a funny cartoon. You get an anniversary card with your words set in the design, portrait, square or wide, ready to print, post or send. Give only the words you want printed on the card: no addresses or contact details are needed. For your partner, your parents' milestone anniversary and friends celebrating theirs. Claude tells you the price first and makes it in your MegaScenes account when you say yes.

**How the files are made:** each anniversary card is generated by an image model. Nothing is made until you say yes, and the price in credits comes first.

Limitations: designs as image files; your text is passed to the model as written, so check the lettering in the result. It does not mail the cards or keep a list of dates. Making a file needs a signed-in MegaScenes account and runs in it; nothing is made until you confirm. Not for sexual or adult content or for real people's likeness. Files are made by generative models and saved in your MegaScenes library.

## Install
In Claude Code:
```
/plugin marketplace add megascenes-app/claude-plugins
/plugin install anniversary-card@megascenes
```

## Connecting
- Anniversary Card Maker talks to the MegaScenes server at `https://megascenes.com/api/claude/anniversary-card/mcp` (Streamable HTTP).
- Connecting opens MegaScenes: sign in, create an account, or continue without one and add your email when you pay (OAuth with PKCE). Then you come straight back to the chat.
- Each anniversary card uses credits from your MegaScenes account and is priced before it runs; new accounts can start with a low-cost trial week.
- The anniversary cards show in a viewer in the chat where Claude supports interactive apps; in Claude Code you get the links.

## Tools
| Tool | What it does |
|---|---|
| `list_anniversary_card_models` | Models for anniversary cards with settings and starting prices. Read-only. |
| `estimate_anniversary_card_cost` | Exact price of one anniversary card before it runs. Read-only. |
| `create_anniversary_card` | Starts one anniversary card in your account. Uses credits; needs sign-in. |
| `get_anniversary_card` | Status and file links. Read-only. |
| `get_account` | Your plan and credit balance. Read-only. |

## Example prompts

## Data and privacy
What you give for the anniversary card (the details you give) goes to MegaScenes, stays in your MegaScenes account with the finished file, and is passed to the model provider that makes it. The plugin has no hooks or local scripts and sends nothing else.
