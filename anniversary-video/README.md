# Anniversary Video Maker

Make a short anniversary video for your partner or your parents: a romantic scene from your description, or a photo of the couple from your MegaScenes library brought to life.

## What it does
Describe the scene and, if you like, the years you are celebrating: the couple dancing in their kitchen, a candlelit dinner, the two of them on a beach at sunset, a walk under falling leaves. Or pick a photo of the two of you from your MegaScenes library and bring it to life. You get a short clip, wide, tall for reels and stories, or square. For anniversary surprises, messages to your partner and a gift for your parents' milestone. Claude tells you the price first and makes it in your MegaScenes account when you say yes.

**How the files are made:** each clip is generated by a video model. Nothing is made until you say yes, and the price in credits comes first.

Limitations: a short clip of a few seconds, silent unless you ask for sound and the model offers it. A photo is optional; if you use one, only the couple from your own photos, with the consent of both, never a famous person. The photo must already be in your MegaScenes library; attachments from the chat are not passed to the plugin. Making a file needs a signed-in MegaScenes account and runs in it; nothing is made until you confirm. Not for sexual or adult content. Files are made by generative models and saved in your MegaScenes library.

## Install
In Claude Code:
```
/plugin marketplace add megascenes-app/claude-plugins
/plugin install anniversary-video@megascenes
```

## Connecting
- Anniversary Video Maker talks to the MegaScenes server at `https://megascenes.com/api/claude/anniversary-video/mcp` (Streamable HTTP).
- Connecting opens MegaScenes: sign in, create an account, or continue without one and add your email when you pay (OAuth with PKCE). Then you come straight back to the chat.
- Each clip uses credits from your MegaScenes account and is priced before it runs; new accounts can start with a low-cost trial week.
- Clips take one to a few minutes: Claude checks on progress, and the clip plays in a viewer in the chat where Claude supports interactive apps (a link in Claude Code).

## Tools
| Tool | What it does |
|---|---|
| `list_anniversary_video_models` | Models for anniversary videos with settings and starting prices. Read-only. |
| `estimate_anniversary_video_cost` | Exact price of one anniversary video before it runs. Read-only. |
| `create_anniversary_video` | Starts one anniversary video in your account. Uses credits; needs sign-in. |
| `get_anniversary_video` | Status and file links. Read-only. |
| `list_my_images` | Your uploads and images made here, to pick the one to start from. Read-only. |
| `get_account` | Your plan and credit balance. Read-only. |

## Example prompts
- "An anniversary video of a couple dancing in their kitchen at night"

- "Bring the photo of us in my MegaScenes library to life"

## Data and privacy
What you give for the clip (the picture you pick from your library, if any, and the details you give) goes to MegaScenes, stays in your MegaScenes account with the finished file, and is passed to the model provider that makes it. The plugin has no hooks or local scripts and sends nothing else.
