# Photo Restoration

Restore old or damaged photos from your MegaScenes library: scratches, tears, creases, stains and faded colors repaired, the same photo with the same people.

## What it does
Pick a scan or a photo of the print from your MegaScenes library and say what to fix: a tear across the corner, water stains, a crease, faded colors, dust and scratches. Keep its own colors or black and white, or also colorize it. You get the same photo, repaired and gently sharpened, with the same composition, people and background. For family albums, old portraits, photos for an anniversary or a memorial, and prints that have faded over the years. Claude tells you the price first and makes it in your MegaScenes account when you say yes.

**How the files are made:** each restored photo is generated by an image model. Nothing is made until you say yes, and the price in credits comes first.

Limitations: the repaired parts are redrawn by the model, so details can differ from the original; if you also colorize it, the colors are a plausible interpretation, not the real ones. Only your own or your family's photos that you have the right to edit, never a famous person. One photo per run, already in your MegaScenes library; attachments from the chat are not passed to the plugin. Making a file needs a signed-in MegaScenes account and runs in it; nothing is made until you confirm. Not for sexual or adult content. Files are made by generative models and saved in your MegaScenes library.

## Install
In Claude Code:
```
/plugin marketplace add megascenes-app/claude-plugins
/plugin install photo-restoration@megascenes
```

## Connecting
- Photo Restoration talks to the MegaScenes server at `https://megascenes.com/api/claude/photo-restoration/mcp` (Streamable HTTP).
- Connecting opens MegaScenes: sign in, create an account, or continue without one and add your email when you pay (OAuth with PKCE). Then you come straight back to the chat.
- Each restored photo uses credits from your MegaScenes account and is priced before it runs; new accounts can start with a low-cost trial week.
- The restored photos show in a viewer in the chat where Claude supports interactive apps; in Claude Code you get the links.

## Tools
| Tool | What it does |
|---|---|
| `list_restored_photo_models` | Models for restored photos with settings and starting prices. Read-only. |
| `estimate_restored_photo_cost` | Exact price of one restored photo before it runs. Read-only. |
| `create_restored_photo` | Starts one restored photo in your account. Uses credits; needs sign-in. |
| `get_restored_photo` | Status and file links. Read-only. |
| `list_my_images` | Your uploads and images made here, to pick the one to start from. Read-only. |
| `get_account` | Your plan and credit balance. Read-only. |

## Example prompts
- "Restore the torn old photo of my grandmother in my MegaScenes library"
- "Fix the water stains and the crease on my latest upload"
- "Restore and colorize the faded family photo in my library"

## Data and privacy
What you give for the restored photo (the picture you pick from your library and the details you give) goes to MegaScenes, stays in your MegaScenes account with the finished file, and is passed to the model provider that makes it. The plugin has no hooks or local scripts and sends nothing else.
