# Christmas Wallpaper Maker

Create Christmas wallpapers and backgrounds for your phone or computer, in the scene and style you choose.

## What it does
Describe the Christmas scene you want behind your apps: a snowy forest at night, a tree full of lights, gingerbread houses, a minimal pattern of stars. You get a wallpaper in the shape you need, tall for a phone or wide for a computer. For phones, laptops, video call backgrounds and holiday screens. Claude tells you the price first and makes it in your MegaScenes account when you say yes.

**How the files are made:** each Christmas wallpaper is generated by an image model. Nothing is made until you say yes, and the price in credits comes first.

Limitations: one image per run; it does not set the wallpaper on your device for you. Making a file needs a signed-in MegaScenes account and runs in it; nothing is made until you confirm. Not for sexual or adult content or for real people's likeness. Files are made by generative models and saved in your MegaScenes library.

## Install
In Claude Code:
```
/plugin marketplace add megascenes-app/claude-plugins
/plugin install christmas-wallpaper@megascenes
```

## Connecting
- Christmas Wallpaper Maker talks to the MegaScenes server at `https://megascenes.com/api/claude/christmas-wallpaper/mcp` (Streamable HTTP).
- Connecting opens MegaScenes: sign in, create an account, or continue without one and add your email when you pay (OAuth with PKCE). Then you come straight back to the chat.
- Each Christmas wallpaper uses credits from your MegaScenes account and is priced before it runs; new accounts can start with a low-cost trial week.
- The Christmas wallpapers show in a viewer in the chat where Claude supports interactive apps; in Claude Code you get the links.

## Tools
| Tool | What it does |
|---|---|
| `list_christmas_wallpaper_models` | Models for Christmas wallpapers with settings and starting prices. Read-only. |
| `estimate_christmas_wallpaper_cost` | Exact price of one Christmas wallpaper before it runs. Read-only. |
| `create_christmas_wallpaper` | Starts one Christmas wallpaper in your account. Uses credits; needs sign-in. |
| `get_christmas_wallpaper` | Status and file links. Read-only. |
| `get_account` | Your plan and credit balance. Read-only. |

## Example prompts
- "A Christmas phone wallpaper of a snowy forest at night with warm lights"
- "A wide Christmas desktop background with a tree full of lights"
- "A minimal Christmas pattern of gold stars on dark green for my phone"

## Data and privacy
What you give for the Christmas wallpaper (the details you give) goes to MegaScenes, stays in your MegaScenes account with the finished file, and is passed to the model provider that makes it. The plugin has no hooks or local scripts and sends nothing else.
