# Halloween Coloring Pages

Create printable Halloween coloring pages with clean black outlines on white: friendly ghosts, pumpkins, black cats and haunted houses, spooky and fun.

## What it does
Name a Halloween subject and how detailed it should be: a friendly ghost in a pumpkin patch, a black cat on a broom, a witch's hat full of candy, a haunted house mandala, in simple bold shapes, medium detail or intricate patterns. You get a coloring page with clean black outlines on white, spooky and fun, never gory. For parents, teachers and anyone who loves to color, for a class activity, a Halloween party table or a cozy October evening. Claude tells you the price first and makes it in your MegaScenes account when you say yes.

**How the files are made:** each Halloween coloring page is generated by an image model. Nothing is made until you say yes, and the price in credits comes first.

Limitations: new pages from a description, without text on the page; it does not turn a photo into a coloring page. Designs are original, not characters or brands owned by others. Making a file needs a signed-in MegaScenes account and runs in it; nothing is made until you confirm. Not for sexual or adult content or for real people's likeness. Files are made by generative models and saved in your MegaScenes library.

## Install
In Claude Code:
```
/plugin marketplace add megascenes-app/claude-plugins
/plugin install halloween-coloring@megascenes
```

## Connecting
- Halloween Coloring Pages talks to the MegaScenes server at `https://megascenes.com/api/claude/halloween-coloring/mcp` (Streamable HTTP).
- Connecting opens MegaScenes: sign in, create an account, or continue without one and add your email when you pay (OAuth with PKCE). Then you come straight back to the chat.
- Each Halloween coloring page uses credits from your MegaScenes account and is priced before it runs; new accounts can start with a low-cost trial week.
- The Halloween coloring pages show in a viewer in the chat where Claude supports interactive apps; in Claude Code you get the links.

## Tools
| Tool | What it does |
|---|---|
| `list_halloween_coloring_page_models` | Models for Halloween coloring pages with settings and starting prices. Read-only. |
| `estimate_halloween_coloring_page_cost` | Exact price of one Halloween coloring page before it runs. Read-only. |
| `create_halloween_coloring_page` | Starts one Halloween coloring page in your account. Uses credits; needs sign-in. |
| `get_halloween_coloring_page` | Status and file links. Read-only. |
| `get_account` | Your plan and credit balance. Read-only. |

## Data and privacy
What you give for the Halloween coloring page (the details you give) goes to MegaScenes, stays in your MegaScenes account with the finished file, and is passed to the model provider that makes it. The plugin has no hooks or local scripts and sends nothing else.
