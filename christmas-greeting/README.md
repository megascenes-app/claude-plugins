# Christmas Greeting Images

Create Merry Christmas images with your own wish written in the picture, ready to share in chats and on social media.

## What it does
Write the wish you want to share, such as Merry Christmas, Happy Holidays or a line of your own, and pick the scene: a glowing tree, a snowy street, ornaments on a branch, a fireplace. You get a festive image with your words in it, ready to send. For group chats, status updates, stories and holiday posts. Claude tells you the price first and makes it in your MegaScenes account when you say yes.

**How the files are made:** each Christmas greeting image is generated by an image model. Nothing is made until you say yes, and the price in credits comes first.

Limitations: one image per run; your text is passed to the model as written, so check the lettering in the result. It does not post or send the image for you. Making a file needs a signed-in MegaScenes account and runs in it; nothing is made until you confirm. Not for sexual or adult content or for real people's likeness. Files are made by generative models and saved in your MegaScenes library.

## Install
In Claude Code:
```
/plugin marketplace add megascenes-app/claude-plugins
/plugin install christmas-greeting@megascenes
```

## Connecting
- Christmas Greeting Images talks to the MegaScenes server at `https://megascenes.com/api/claude/christmas-greeting/mcp` (Streamable HTTP).
- Connecting opens MegaScenes: sign in, create an account, or continue without one and add your email when you pay (OAuth with PKCE). Then you come straight back to the chat.
- Each Christmas greeting image uses credits from your MegaScenes account and is priced before it runs; new accounts can start with a low-cost trial week.
- The Christmas greeting images show in a viewer in the chat where Claude supports interactive apps; in Claude Code you get the links.

## Tools
| Tool | What it does |
|---|---|
| `list_christmas_greeting_models` | Models for Christmas greeting images with settings and starting prices. Read-only. |
| `estimate_christmas_greeting_cost` | Exact price of one Christmas greeting image before it runs. Read-only. |
| `create_christmas_greeting` | Starts one Christmas greeting image in your account. Uses credits; needs sign-in. |
| `get_christmas_greeting` | Status and file links. Read-only. |
| `get_account` | Your plan and credit balance. Read-only. |

## Example prompts
- "A Merry Christmas image with a glowing tree and falling snow"
- "A Happy Holidays image with gold ornaments for my group chat"
- "A cozy fireplace image with the words Warm wishes this Christmas"

## Data and privacy
What you give for the Christmas greeting image (the details you give) goes to MegaScenes, stays in your MegaScenes account with the finished file, and is passed to the model provider that makes it. The plugin has no hooks or local scripts and sends nothing else.
