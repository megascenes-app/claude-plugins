# Lunar New Year Greetings

Create Lunar New Year greeting images with your own wishes written in the picture, over red lanterns, plum blossoms or the animal of the year, drawn with respect for the tradition.

## What it does
Write the wishes you want to send, such as Happy Lunar New Year 2027 or a line of your own, add the animal of the year if you like, such as the Goat for 2027, and pick the scene and the look: red lanterns at night, plum blossoms, fireworks over a city, a family dinner table, as elegant red and gold, paper-cut art, watercolor, ink painting or modern minimal. You get a festive image with your words in it and the tradition's symbols drawn respectfully, with no caricatures, square, tall for stories and status, or wide. For family chats, friends, colleagues and Lunar New Year posts. Claude tells you the price first and makes it in your MegaScenes account when you say yes.

**How the files are made:** each Lunar New Year greeting image is generated by an image model. Nothing is made until you say yes, and the price in credits comes first.

Limitations: one image per run; your text is passed to the model as written, so check the lettering in the result. It does not post or send the image for you. Making a file needs a signed-in MegaScenes account and runs in it; nothing is made until you confirm. Not for sexual or adult content or for real people's likeness. Files are made by generative models and saved in your MegaScenes library.

## Install
In Claude Code:
```
/plugin marketplace add megascenes-app/claude-plugins
/plugin install lunar-new-year-greeting@megascenes
```

## Connecting
- Lunar New Year Greetings talks to the MegaScenes server at `https://megascenes.com/api/claude/lunar-new-year-greeting/mcp` (Streamable HTTP).
- Connecting opens MegaScenes: sign in, create an account, or continue without one and add your email when you pay (OAuth with PKCE). Then you come straight back to the chat.
- Each Lunar New Year greeting image uses credits from your MegaScenes account and is priced before it runs; new accounts can start with a low-cost trial week.
- The Lunar New Year greeting images show in a viewer in the chat where Claude supports interactive apps; in Claude Code you get the links.

## Tools
| Tool | What it does |
|---|---|
| `list_lunar_new_year_greeting_models` | Models for Lunar New Year greeting images with settings and starting prices. Read-only. |
| `estimate_lunar_new_year_greeting_cost` | Exact price of one Lunar New Year greeting image before it runs. Read-only. |
| `create_lunar_new_year_greeting` | Starts one Lunar New Year greeting image in your account. Uses credits; needs sign-in. |
| `get_lunar_new_year_greeting` | Status and file links. Read-only. |
| `get_account` | Your plan and credit balance. Read-only. |

## Data and privacy
What you give for the Lunar New Year greeting image (the details you give) goes to MegaScenes, stays in your MegaScenes account with the finished file, and is passed to the model provider that makes it. The plugin has no hooks or local scripts and sends nothing else.
