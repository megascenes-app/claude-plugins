# Pet Memorial Portraits

Remember a pet you lost with a memorial portrait from its photo in your MegaScenes library: in a sunlit meadow, under a rainbow or among the stars, keeping its breed and markings.

## What it does
Pick a photo of your dog, cat or any animal from your MegaScenes library and choose the scene and the style: a sunlit meadow, a rainbow bridge, among the stars, with soft angel wings, in soft watercolor, oil painting, pastel or photo. Add its name if you like. You get a gentle portrait that keeps its breed, colors and markings, portrait or square. For a print to frame, a keepsake, a card for the family or a post to remember them. Claude tells you the price first and makes it in your MegaScenes account when you say yes.

**How the files are made:** each pet memorial portrait is generated by an image model. Nothing is made until you say yes, and the price in credits comes first.

Limitations: one pet photo per portrait, already in your MegaScenes library; attachments from the chat are not passed to the plugin. A name is passed to the model as written, so check the lettering in the result. Making a file needs a signed-in MegaScenes account and runs in it; nothing is made until you confirm. Not for sexual or adult content or for real people's likeness. Files are made by generative models and saved in your MegaScenes library.

## Install
In Claude Code:
```
/plugin marketplace add megascenes-app/claude-plugins
/plugin install pet-memorial-portrait@megascenes
```

## Connecting
- Pet Memorial Portraits talks to the MegaScenes server at `https://megascenes.com/api/claude/pet-memorial-portrait/mcp` (Streamable HTTP).
- Connecting opens MegaScenes: sign in, create an account, or continue without one and add your email when you pay (OAuth with PKCE). Then you come straight back to the chat.
- Each pet memorial portrait uses credits from your MegaScenes account and is priced before it runs; new accounts can start with a low-cost trial week.
- The pet memorial portraits show in a viewer in the chat where Claude supports interactive apps; in Claude Code you get the links.

## Tools
| Tool | What it does |
|---|---|
| `list_pet_memorial_portrait_models` | Models for pet memorial portraits with settings and starting prices. Read-only. |
| `estimate_pet_memorial_portrait_cost` | Exact price of one pet memorial portrait before it runs. Read-only. |
| `create_pet_memorial_portrait` | Starts one pet memorial portrait in your account. Uses credits; needs sign-in. |
| `get_pet_memorial_portrait` | Status and file links. Read-only. |
| `list_my_images` | Your uploads and images made here, to pick the one to start from. Read-only. |
| `get_account` | Your plan and credit balance. Read-only. |

## Data and privacy
What you give for the pet memorial portrait (the picture you pick from your library and the details you give) goes to MegaScenes, stays in your MegaScenes account with the finished file, and is passed to the model provider that makes it. The plugin has no hooks or local scripts and sends nothing else.
