# Father's Day Song Maker

Create an original Father's Day song with vocals for your dad or grandpa: his name, your memories together and what he means to you.

## What it does
Give the name to sing, a few memories to sing about and the style: country, classic rock, acoustic, blues or pop, with a male or female lead, a duet or a choir. You get an original Father's Day song with vocals as an audio file, with lyrics written from your memories or the lyrics you give. The voice sings the name you give. Give only the name and the details you want sung: no address or contact details are needed. To play at a Father's Day barbecue, send in the family chat or add to a video for him. Claude tells you the price first and makes it in your MegaScenes account when you say yes.

**How the files are made:** each Father's Day song is generated by an audio model. Nothing is made until you say yes, and the price in credits comes first.

Limitations: original songs; it does not imitate a real singer's voice or copy an existing song or artist. Making a file needs a signed-in MegaScenes account and runs in it; nothing is made until you confirm. Not for sexual or adult content or for real people's likeness. Files are made by generative models and saved in your MegaScenes library.

## Install
In Claude Code:
```
/plugin marketplace add megascenes-app/claude-plugins
/plugin install fathers-day-song@megascenes
```

## Connecting
- Father's Day Song Maker talks to the MegaScenes server at `https://megascenes.com/api/claude/fathers-day-song/mcp` (Streamable HTTP).
- Connecting opens MegaScenes: sign in, create an account, or continue without one and add your email when you pay (OAuth with PKCE). Then you come straight back to the chat.
- Each Father's Day song uses credits from your MegaScenes account and is priced before it runs; new accounts can start with a low-cost trial week.
- The Father's Day song plays in a viewer in the chat where Claude supports interactive apps; in Claude Code you get the link.

## Tools
| Tool | What it does |
|---|---|
| `list_fathers_day_song_models` | Models for Father's Day songs with settings and starting prices. Read-only. |
| `estimate_fathers_day_song_cost` | Exact price of one Father's Day song before it runs. Read-only. |
| `create_fathers_day_song` | Starts one Father's Day song in your account. Uses credits; needs sign-in. |
| `get_fathers_day_song` | Status and file links. Read-only. |
| `get_account` | Your plan and credit balance. Read-only. |

## Example prompts
- "A country song for Dad about our fishing trips and his terrible jokes"
- "A classic rock song for Papa Joe about fixing the old car together"
- "An acoustic Father's Day ballad for my dad, sung as a duet"

## Data and privacy
What you give for the Father's Day song (the details you give) goes to MegaScenes, stays in your MegaScenes account with the finished file, and is passed to the model provider that makes it. The plugin has no hooks or local scripts and sends nothing else.
