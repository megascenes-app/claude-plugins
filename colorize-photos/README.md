# Colorize Photos

Bring black and white photos from your MegaScenes library to color: family photos, old portraits, weddings and places, with the same composition and the same people.

## What it does
Pick a black and white photo from your MegaScenes library, a scan or a photo of the print, and add any colors you know: her dress was light blue, the car was red, the leaves were autumn gold. You get the same photo in natural color, with the same composition, people, faces and background. For family albums, old portraits of your grandparents, wedding photos from decades ago and pictures of the places you come from. Claude tells you the price first and makes it in your MegaScenes account when you say yes.

**How the files are made:** each colorized photo is generated by an image model. Nothing is made until you say yes, and the price in credits comes first.

Limitations: the colors are a plausible interpretation, not the real ones of the day. Only your own or your family's photos that you have the right to edit, never a famous person. One photo per run, already in your MegaScenes library; attachments from the chat are not passed to the plugin. Making a file needs a signed-in MegaScenes account and runs in it; nothing is made until you confirm. Not for sexual or adult content. Files are made by generative models and saved in your MegaScenes library.

## Install
In Claude Code:
```
/plugin marketplace add megascenes-app/claude-plugins
/plugin install colorize-photos@megascenes
```

## Connecting
- Colorize Photos talks to the MegaScenes server at `https://megascenes.com/api/claude/colorize-photos/mcp` (Streamable HTTP).
- Connecting opens MegaScenes: sign in, create an account, or continue without one and add your email when you pay (OAuth with PKCE). Then you come straight back to the chat.
- Each colorized photo uses credits from your MegaScenes account and is priced before it runs; new accounts can start with a low-cost trial week.
- The colorized photos show in a viewer in the chat where Claude supports interactive apps; in Claude Code you get the links.

## Tools
| Tool | What it does |
|---|---|
| `list_colorized_photo_models` | Models for colorized photos with settings and starting prices. Read-only. |
| `estimate_colorized_photo_cost` | Exact price of one colorized photo before it runs. Read-only. |
| `create_colorized_photo` | Starts one colorized photo in your account. Uses credits; needs sign-in. |
| `get_colorized_photo` | Status and file links. Read-only. |
| `list_my_images` | Your uploads and images made here, to pick the one to start from. Read-only. |
| `get_account` | Your plan and credit balance. Read-only. |

## Data and privacy
What you give for the colorized photo (the picture you pick from your library and the details you give) goes to MegaScenes, stays in your MegaScenes account with the finished file, and is passed to the model provider that makes it. The plugin has no hooks or local scripts and sends nothing else.
