# Thanksgiving Card Maker

Design Thanksgiving cards with your greeting, your message and your signature in the design, in the look you choose.

## What it does
Give the main line, who the card is for, a short message and how you sign it, and pick the look: autumn leaves, a rustic harvest table, pumpkins and corn, warm watercolor, minimal. You get a Thanksgiving card with your words set in the design, portrait, square or wide, ready to print, post or send. Give only the words you want printed on the card: no addresses or contact details are needed. For family, friends, clients and everyone you are grateful for this year. Claude tells you the price first and makes it in your MegaScenes account when you say yes.

**How the files are made:** each Thanksgiving card is generated by an image model. Nothing is made until you say yes, and the price in credits comes first.

Limitations: designs as image files; your text is passed to the model as written, so check the lettering in the result. It does not mail the cards. Making a file needs a signed-in MegaScenes account and runs in it; nothing is made until you confirm. Not for sexual or adult content or for real people's likeness. Files are made by generative models and saved in your MegaScenes library.

## Install
In Claude Code:
```
/plugin marketplace add megascenes-app/claude-plugins
/plugin install thanksgiving-card@megascenes
```

## Connecting
- Thanksgiving Card Maker talks to the MegaScenes server at `https://megascenes.com/api/claude/thanksgiving-card/mcp` (Streamable HTTP).
- Connecting opens MegaScenes: sign in, create an account, or continue without one and add your email when you pay (OAuth with PKCE). Then you come straight back to the chat.
- Each Thanksgiving card uses credits from your MegaScenes account and is priced before it runs; new accounts can start with a low-cost trial week.
- The Thanksgiving cards show in a viewer in the chat where Claude supports interactive apps; in Claude Code you get the links.

## Tools
| Tool | What it does |
|---|---|
| `list_thanksgiving_card_models` | Models for Thanksgiving cards with settings and starting prices. Read-only. |
| `estimate_thanksgiving_card_cost` | Exact price of one Thanksgiving card before it runs. Read-only. |
| `create_thanksgiving_card` | Starts one Thanksgiving card in your account. Uses credits; needs sign-in. |
| `get_thanksgiving_card` | Status and file links. Read-only. |
| `get_account` | Your plan and credit balance. Read-only. |

## Example prompts
- "A Thanksgiving card with autumn leaves that says Happy Thanksgiving, signed With love, the Johnsons"
- "A warm watercolor Thanksgiving card for Grandma and Grandpa: Grateful for you"
- "A minimal Thanksgiving card for my clients: Thankful for a great 2026"

## Data and privacy
What you give for the Thanksgiving card (the details you give) goes to MegaScenes, stays in your MegaScenes account with the finished file, and is passed to the model provider that makes it. The plugin has no hooks or local scripts and sends nothing else.
