# Valentine Coloring Pages

Create printable Valentine's Day coloring pages with clean black outlines on white: hearts, roses, animals in love, love letters and mandalas, simple or detailed.

## What it does
Name a Valentine's subject and how detailed it should be: two bears holding a heart, a heart mandala, a bouquet of roses, love letters and envelopes, two owls sharing a heart, in simple bold shapes, medium detail or intricate patterns. You get a coloring page with clean black outlines on white. For parents, teachers and adults who love to color, for a class activity, a quiet evening or a handmade gift. Claude tells you the price first and makes it in your MegaScenes account when you say yes.

**How the files are made:** each Valentine coloring page is generated by an image model. Nothing is made until you say yes, and the price in credits comes first.

Limitations: new pages from a description, without text on the page; it does not turn a photo into a coloring page. Designs are original, not characters or brands owned by others. Making a file needs a signed-in MegaScenes account and runs in it; nothing is made until you confirm. Not for sexual or adult content or for real people's likeness. Files are made by generative models and saved in your MegaScenes library.

## Install
In Claude Code:
```
/plugin marketplace add megascenes-app/claude-plugins
/plugin install valentine-coloring@megascenes
```

## Connecting
- Valentine Coloring Pages talks to the MegaScenes server at `https://megascenes.com/api/claude/valentine-coloring/mcp` (Streamable HTTP).
- Connecting opens MegaScenes: sign in, create an account, or continue without one and add your email when you pay (OAuth with PKCE). Then you come straight back to the chat.
- Each Valentine coloring page uses credits from your MegaScenes account and is priced before it runs; new accounts can start with a low-cost trial week.
- The Valentine coloring pages show in a viewer in the chat where Claude supports interactive apps; in Claude Code you get the links.

## Tools
| Tool | What it does |
|---|---|
| `list_valentine_coloring_page_models` | Models for Valentine coloring pages with settings and starting prices. Read-only. |
| `estimate_valentine_coloring_page_cost` | Exact price of one Valentine coloring page before it runs. Read-only. |
| `create_valentine_coloring_page` | Starts one Valentine coloring page in your account. Uses credits; needs sign-in. |
| `get_valentine_coloring_page` | Status and file links. Read-only. |
| `get_account` | Your plan and credit balance. Read-only. |

## Example prompts

## Data and privacy
What you give for the Valentine coloring page (the details you give) goes to MegaScenes, stays in your MegaScenes account with the finished file, and is passed to the model provider that makes it. The plugin has no hooks or local scripts and sends nothing else.
