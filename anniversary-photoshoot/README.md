# Anniversary Photoshoot

Create anniversary photos of the two of you from your own photos in your MegaScenes library: the same faces at a candlelit dinner, in Paris at night or recreating your wedding day.

## What it does
Pick one photo of the two of you, or one photo of each, from your MegaScenes library and choose the setting, the outfits and the style: a candlelit dinner, Paris at night, a beach at sunset, a recreation of your wedding day, in elegant evening wear or matching white outfits, romantic editorial, candid film, black and white or fine art. You get a picture that keeps both faces, in the scene you chose, portrait, landscape or tall for stories. For anniversary posts, prints, surprises and photo albums. Claude tells you the price first and makes it in your MegaScenes account when you say yes.

**How the files are made:** each anniversary photo is generated by an image model. Nothing is made until you say yes, and the price in credits comes first.

Limitations: only the couple from your own photos, with the consent of both, never a famous person; no swimwear, underwear or sensual shots. One or two photos, already in your MegaScenes library; attachments from the chat are not passed to the plugin. Making a file needs a signed-in MegaScenes account and runs in it; nothing is made until you confirm. Not for sexual or adult content. Files are made by generative models and saved in your MegaScenes library.

## Install
In Claude Code:
```
/plugin marketplace add megascenes-app/claude-plugins
/plugin install anniversary-photoshoot@megascenes
```

## Connecting
- Anniversary Photoshoot talks to the MegaScenes server at `https://megascenes.com/api/claude/anniversary-photoshoot/mcp` (Streamable HTTP).
- Connecting opens MegaScenes: sign in, create an account, or continue without one and add your email when you pay (OAuth with PKCE). Then you come straight back to the chat.
- Each anniversary photo uses credits from your MegaScenes account and is priced before it runs; new accounts can start with a low-cost trial week.
- The anniversary photos show in a viewer in the chat where Claude supports interactive apps; in Claude Code you get the links.

## Tools
| Tool | What it does |
|---|---|
| `list_anniversary_photoshoot_models` | Models for anniversary photos with settings and starting prices. Read-only. |
| `estimate_anniversary_photoshoot_cost` | Exact price of one anniversary photo before it runs. Read-only. |
| `create_anniversary_photoshoot` | Starts one anniversary photo in your account. Uses credits; needs sign-in. |
| `get_anniversary_photoshoot` | Status and file links. Read-only. |
| `list_my_images` | Your uploads and images made here, to pick the ones to start from. Read-only. |
| `get_account` | Your plan and credit balance. Read-only. |

## Example prompts
- "Make an anniversary photo of us at a candlelit dinner from my latest MegaScenes photo"
- "Recreate our wedding day with the two of us, in black and white"
- "Put us on a Paris street at night in elegant evening wear"

## Data and privacy
What you give for the anniversary photo (the pictures you pick from your library and the details you give) goes to MegaScenes, stays in your MegaScenes account with the finished file, and is passed to the model provider that makes it. The plugin has no hooks or local scripts and sends nothing else.
