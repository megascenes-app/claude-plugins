# Birthday Printables

Design birthday party printables in one matching theme: cupcake toppers, gift tags, food labels, place cards and signs, as a sheet to print and cut.

## What it does
Say what you need and the theme: a sheet of cupcake toppers, gift tags, food labels, place cards, a cake topper, a welcome sign or a thank-you card, in pastel rainbow, jungle safari, black and gold or outer space. You get a sheet with the name and the words you gave, to print at home and cut out. Give only the words you want printed: no guest list or contact details are needed. For parents and hosts getting ready for a birthday party. Claude tells you the price first and makes it in your MegaScenes account when you say yes.

**How the files are made:** each birthday printable is generated by an image model. Nothing is made until you say yes, and the price in credits comes first.

Limitations: one printable sheet per run, as an image file; your text is passed to the model as written, so check the lettering in the result. It does not print or cut the sheet. Party themes are original, not characters or brands owned by others. Making a file needs a signed-in MegaScenes account and runs in it; nothing is made until you confirm. Not for sexual or adult content or for real people's likeness. Files are made by generative models and saved in your MegaScenes library.

## Install
In Claude Code:
```
/plugin marketplace add megascenes-app/claude-plugins
/plugin install birthday-printables@megascenes
```

## Connecting
- Birthday Printables talks to the MegaScenes server at `https://megascenes.com/api/claude/birthday-printables/mcp` (Streamable HTTP).
- Connecting opens MegaScenes: sign in, create an account, or continue without one and add your email when you pay (OAuth with PKCE). Then you come straight back to the chat.
- Each birthday printable uses credits from your MegaScenes account and is priced before it runs; new accounts can start with a low-cost trial week.
- The birthday printables show in a viewer in the chat where Claude supports interactive apps; in Claude Code you get the links.

## Tools
| Tool | What it does |
|---|---|
| `list_birthday_printable_models` | Models for birthday printables with settings and starting prices. Read-only. |
| `estimate_birthday_printable_cost` | Exact price of one birthday printable before it runs. Read-only. |
| `create_birthday_printable` | Starts one birthday printable in your account. Uses credits; needs sign-in. |
| `get_birthday_printable` | Status and file links. Read-only. |
| `get_account` | Your plan and credit balance. Read-only. |

## Example prompts
- "A sheet of 12 cupcake toppers in a jungle safari theme"
- "Food labels for Popcorn, Lemonade and Cake in pastel rainbow"
- "A welcome sign for a black and gold birthday party"

## Data and privacy
What you give for the birthday printable (the details you give) goes to MegaScenes, stays in your MegaScenes account with the finished file, and is passed to the model provider that makes it. The plugin has no hooks or local scripts and sends nothing else.
