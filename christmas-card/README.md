# Christmas Card Maker

Design Christmas cards with your own message and names in the design, in the style you choose.

## What it does
Give the message, who the card is from and the look you want: classic red and gold, snowy village, cozy watercolor, modern minimal. You get a Christmas card with your words set in the design, ready to print, post or send. Give only the words you want printed on the card: no addresses or contact details are needed. For family cards, office greetings and holiday posts. Claude tells you the price first and makes it in your MegaScenes account when you say yes.

**How the files are made:** each Christmas card is generated by an image model. Nothing is made until you say yes, and the price in credits comes first.

Limitations: designs as image files; your text is passed to the model as written, so check the lettering in the result. It does not mail the cards or keep address lists. Making a file needs a signed-in MegaScenes account and runs in it; nothing is made until you confirm. Not for sexual or adult content or for real people's likeness. Files are made by generative models and saved in your MegaScenes library.

## Install
In Claude Code:
```
/plugin marketplace add megascenes-app/claude-plugins
/plugin install christmas-card@megascenes
```

## Connecting
- Christmas Card Maker talks to the MegaScenes server at `https://megascenes.com/api/claude/christmas-card/mcp` (Streamable HTTP).
- Connecting opens MegaScenes: sign in, create an account, or continue without one and add your email when you pay (OAuth with PKCE). Then you come straight back to the chat.
- Each Christmas card uses credits from your MegaScenes account and is priced before it runs; new accounts can start with a low-cost trial week.
- The Christmas cards show in a viewer in the chat where Claude supports interactive apps; in Claude Code you get the links.

## Tools
| Tool | What it does |
|---|---|
| `list_christmas_card_models` | Models for Christmas cards with settings and starting prices. Read-only. |
| `estimate_christmas_card_cost` | Exact price of one Christmas card before it runs. Read-only. |
| `create_christmas_card` | Starts one Christmas card in your account. Uses credits; needs sign-in. |
| `get_christmas_card` | Status and file links. Read-only. |
| `get_account` | Your plan and credit balance. Read-only. |

## Example prompts
- "A classic Christmas card with snowy pines that says Merry Christmas from the Rossi family"
- "A modern minimal Christmas card for my team: Happy Holidays and thank you for this year"
- "A watercolor Christmas card with a cozy cabin and the words Peace and Joy"

## Data and privacy
What you give for the Christmas card (the details you give) goes to MegaScenes, stays in your MegaScenes account with the finished file, and is passed to the model provider that makes it. The plugin has no hooks or local scripts and sends nothing else.
