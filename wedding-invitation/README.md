# Wedding Invitations

Design wedding invitations, save the dates, RSVP and thank-you cards with the couple's names, the date and the venue, in the style you choose.

## What it does
Give the couple's names, the date and the venue you want printed, the card and the style: elegant floral, minimal modern, rustic, boho, classic, gold foil, watercolor. Choose an invitation, a save the date, an RSVP card or a thank-you card. You get the card with your words set in the design, portrait to print or tall to send from your phone. Give only the words you want printed: no guest list or contact details are needed. For couples, families and wedding organizers. Claude tells you the price first and makes it in your MegaScenes account when you say yes.

**How the files are made:** each wedding invitation is generated by an image model. Nothing is made until you say yes, and the price in credits comes first.

Limitations: designs as image files; your text is passed to the model as written, so check the lettering in the result. It does not send the invitations, keep guest lists or collect replies. Making a file needs a signed-in MegaScenes account and runs in it; nothing is made until you confirm. Not for sexual or adult content or for real people's likeness. Files are made by generative models and saved in your MegaScenes library.

## Install
In Claude Code:
```
/plugin marketplace add megascenes-app/claude-plugins
/plugin install wedding-invitation@megascenes
```

## Connecting
- Wedding Invitations talks to the MegaScenes server at `https://megascenes.com/api/claude/wedding-invitation/mcp` (Streamable HTTP).
- Connecting opens MegaScenes: sign in, create an account, or continue without one and add your email when you pay (OAuth with PKCE). Then you come straight back to the chat.
- Each wedding invitation uses credits from your MegaScenes account and is priced before it runs; new accounts can start with a low-cost trial week.
- The wedding invitations show in a viewer in the chat where Claude supports interactive apps; in Claude Code you get the links.

## Tools
| Tool | What it does |
|---|---|
| `list_wedding_invitation_models` | Models for wedding invitations with settings and starting prices. Read-only. |
| `estimate_wedding_invitation_cost` | Exact price of one wedding invitation before it runs. Read-only. |
| `create_wedding_invitation` | Starts one wedding invitation in your account. Uses credits; needs sign-in. |
| `get_wedding_invitation` | Status and file links. Read-only. |
| `get_account` | Your plan and credit balance. Read-only. |

## Data and privacy
What you give for the wedding invitation (the details you give) goes to MegaScenes, stays in your MegaScenes account with the finished file, and is passed to the model provider that makes it. The plugin has no hooks or local scripts and sends nothing else.
