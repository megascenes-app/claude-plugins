# Song for Your Child

Create an original song with vocals for your son or daughter, with their name and the things they love, upbeat to dance to or gentle to sing together.

## What it does
Give the name to sing, the things they love, such as dinosaurs, painting, swimming or the family dog, and the style: happy pop, a sing-along, rock, acoustic, reggae, with a female or male lead or a duet. You get an original song with vocals as an audio file, with lyrics written from your details or the lyrics you give. The voice sings the name you give, clearly in the chorus. Give only the first name and the things you want sung: no address or other personal details are needed. For parents and grandparents, for a birthday, a car trip, a new brother or sister, or just to sing together. Claude tells you the price first and makes it in your MegaScenes account when you say yes.

**How the files are made:** each song for your child is generated by an audio model. Nothing is made until you say yes, and the price in credits comes first.

Limitations: original songs; it does not imitate a real singer's voice or copy an existing song or artist, and the words never use characters or brands owned by others. Making a file needs a signed-in MegaScenes account and runs in it; nothing is made until you confirm. Not for sexual or adult content or for real people's likeness. Files are made by generative models and saved in your MegaScenes library.

## Install
In Claude Code:
```
/plugin marketplace add megascenes-app/claude-plugins
/plugin install song-for-child@megascenes
```

## Connecting
- Song for Your Child talks to the MegaScenes server at `https://megascenes.com/api/claude/song-for-child/mcp` (Streamable HTTP).
- Connecting opens MegaScenes: sign in, create an account, or continue without one and add your email when you pay (OAuth with PKCE). Then you come straight back to the chat.
- Each song for your child uses credits from your MegaScenes account and is priced before it runs; new accounts can start with a low-cost trial week.
- The song for your child plays in a viewer in the chat where Claude supports interactive apps; in Claude Code you get the link.

## Tools
| Tool | What it does |
|---|---|
| `list_child_song_models` | Models for songs for your child with settings and starting prices. Read-only. |
| `estimate_child_song_cost` | Exact price of one song for your child before it runs. Read-only. |
| `create_child_song` | Starts one song for your child in your account. Uses credits; needs sign-in. |
| `get_child_song` | Status and file links. Read-only. |
| `get_account` | Your plan and credit balance. Read-only. |

## Example prompts
- "A happy pop song for Mia, who loves dinosaurs and painting"
- "A sing-along song for my son Leo about our dog Max"
- "A gentle acoustic song for my daughter Sofia to sing together"

## Data and privacy
What you give for the song for your child (the details you give) goes to MegaScenes, stays in your MegaScenes account with the finished file, and is passed to the model provider that makes it. The plugin has no hooks or local scripts and sends nothing else.
