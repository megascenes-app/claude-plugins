# Birthday Coloring Pages

Create printable birthday coloring pages with clean black outlines on white: cakes, balloons, presents and party animals, with a name if you like.

## What it does
Name a birthday subject and how detailed it should be: a dinosaur with a party hat, a big cake with candles, balloons and presents, a parade of party animals, in simple bold shapes, medium detail or intricate patterns. Add a name to appear in outline letters. You get a coloring page with clean black outlines on white. For parents and hosts preparing the party table or a party favor, and for anyone who colors to relax. Claude tells you the price first and makes it in your MegaScenes account when you say yes.

**How the files are made:** each birthday coloring page is generated by an image model. Nothing is made until you say yes, and the price in credits comes first.

Limitations: new pages from a description; it does not turn a photo into a coloring page. A name is passed to the model as written, so check the lettering in the result. Themes are original, not characters or brands owned by others. Making a file needs a signed-in MegaScenes account and runs in it; nothing is made until you confirm. Not for sexual or adult content or for real people's likeness. Files are made by generative models and saved in your MegaScenes library.

## Install
In Claude Code:
```
/plugin marketplace add megascenes-app/claude-plugins
/plugin install birthday-coloring@megascenes
```

## Connecting
- Birthday Coloring Pages talks to the MegaScenes server at `https://megascenes.com/api/claude/birthday-coloring/mcp` (Streamable HTTP).
- Connecting opens MegaScenes: sign in, create an account, or continue without one and add your email when you pay (OAuth with PKCE). Then you come straight back to the chat.
- Each birthday coloring page uses credits from your MegaScenes account and is priced before it runs; new accounts can start with a low-cost trial week.
- The birthday coloring pages show in a viewer in the chat where Claude supports interactive apps; in Claude Code you get the links.

## Tools
| Tool | What it does |
|---|---|
| `list_birthday_coloring_page_models` | Models for birthday coloring pages with settings and starting prices. Read-only. |
| `estimate_birthday_coloring_page_cost` | Exact price of one birthday coloring page before it runs. Read-only. |
| `create_birthday_coloring_page` | Starts one birthday coloring page in your account. Uses credits; needs sign-in. |
| `get_birthday_coloring_page` | Status and file links. Read-only. |
| `get_account` | Your plan and credit balance. Read-only. |

## Example prompts
- "A birthday coloring page of a dinosaur with a party hat, simple shapes"
- "A detailed birthday cake mandala coloring page for adults"
- "A coloring page of balloons and presents with the name Mia"

## Data and privacy
What you give for the birthday coloring page (the details you give) goes to MegaScenes, stays in your MegaScenes account with the finished file, and is passed to the model provider that makes it. The plugin has no hooks or local scripts and sends nothing else.
