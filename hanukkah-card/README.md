# Hanukkah Card Maker

Design Hanukkah cards with your greeting, your message and your signature in the design, with the menorah, a dreidel or the Star of David drawn with care.

## What it does
Write the main line, who the card is for, a short message and how you sign it, in your own words, and pick the look: blue and silver, a glowing menorah, gold and white, watercolor, modern minimal. You get a Hanukkah card with your words set in the design and the holiday's traditional symbols drawn respectfully, portrait, square or wide, ready to print, post or send. Give only the words you want printed on the card: no addresses or contact details are needed. For family, friends and colleagues during the eight nights of Hanukkah. Claude tells you the price first and makes it in your MegaScenes account when you say yes.

**How the files are made:** each Hanukkah card is generated by an image model. Nothing is made until you say yes, and the price in credits comes first.

Limitations: designs as image files; your text is passed to the model as written, so check the lettering in the result. It does not mail the cards. Making a file needs a signed-in MegaScenes account and runs in it; nothing is made until you confirm. Not for sexual or adult content or for real people's likeness. Files are made by generative models and saved in your MegaScenes library.

## Install
In Claude Code:
```
/plugin marketplace add megascenes-app/claude-plugins
/plugin install hanukkah-card@megascenes
```

## Connecting
- Hanukkah Card Maker talks to the MegaScenes server at `https://megascenes.com/api/claude/hanukkah-card/mcp` (Streamable HTTP).
- Connecting opens MegaScenes: sign in, create an account, or continue without one and add your email when you pay (OAuth with PKCE). Then you come straight back to the chat.
- Each Hanukkah card uses credits from your MegaScenes account and is priced before it runs; new accounts can start with a low-cost trial week.
- The Hanukkah cards show in a viewer in the chat where Claude supports interactive apps; in Claude Code you get the links.

## Tools
| Tool | What it does |
|---|---|
| `list_hanukkah_card_models` | Models for Hanukkah cards with settings and starting prices. Read-only. |
| `estimate_hanukkah_card_cost` | Exact price of one Hanukkah card before it runs. Read-only. |
| `create_hanukkah_card` | Starts one Hanukkah card in your account. Uses credits; needs sign-in. |
| `get_hanukkah_card` | Status and file links. Read-only. |
| `get_account` | Your plan and credit balance. Read-only. |

## Data and privacy
What you give for the Hanukkah card (the details you give) goes to MegaScenes, stays in your MegaScenes account with the finished file, and is passed to the model provider that makes it. The plugin has no hooks or local scripts and sends nothing else.
