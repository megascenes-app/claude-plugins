# New Year Card Maker

Design New Year cards with your own wish and names in the design, for 2027 and beyond, in the style you choose.

## What it does
Give the wish, who the card is from and the look you want: gold fireworks, a midnight countdown, champagne and confetti, a calm winter sunrise. You get a New Year card with your words set in the design, ready to print, post or send. Give only the words you want on the card: no addresses or contact details are needed. For friends, family, clients and team greetings. Claude tells you the price first and makes it in your MegaScenes account when you say yes.

**How the files are made:** each New Year card is generated by an image model. Nothing is made until you say yes, and the price in credits comes first.

Limitations: designs as image files; your text is passed to the model as written, so check the lettering in the result. It does not mail the cards. Making a file needs a signed-in MegaScenes account and runs in it; nothing is made until you confirm. Not for sexual or adult content or for real people's likeness. Files are made by generative models and saved in your MegaScenes library.

## Install
In Claude Code:
```
/plugin marketplace add megascenes-app/claude-plugins
/plugin install new-year-card@megascenes
```

## Connecting
- New Year Card Maker talks to the MegaScenes server at `https://megascenes.com/api/claude/new-year-card/mcp` (Streamable HTTP).
- Connecting opens MegaScenes: sign in, create an account, or continue without one and add your email when you pay (OAuth with PKCE). Then you come straight back to the chat.
- Each New Year card uses credits from your MegaScenes account and is priced before it runs; new accounts can start with a low-cost trial week.
- The New Year cards show in a viewer in the chat where Claude supports interactive apps; in Claude Code you get the links.

## Tools
| Tool | What it does |
|---|---|
| `list_new_year_card_models` | Models for New Year cards with settings and starting prices. Read-only. |
| `estimate_new_year_card_cost` | Exact price of one New Year card before it runs. Read-only. |
| `create_new_year_card` | Starts one New Year card in your account. Uses credits; needs sign-in. |
| `get_new_year_card` | Status and file links. Read-only. |
| `get_account` | Your plan and credit balance. Read-only. |

## Data and privacy
What you give for the New Year card (the details you give) goes to MegaScenes, stays in your MegaScenes account with the finished file, and is passed to the model provider that makes it. The plugin has no hooks or local scripts and sends nothing else.
