# Christmas Pet Portraits

Turn a photo of your pet from your MegaScenes library into a Christmas portrait: a Santa hat, a cozy sweater, by the tree, keeping its breed and markings.

## What it does
Pick a photo of your dog, cat or any animal from your MegaScenes library and choose the Christmas look: a Santa hat, a knitted sweater, reindeer antlers, sleeping by the tree, a painted holiday portrait. You get a portrait that keeps its breed, colors and markings. For holiday cards, gifts, prints and pet profiles. Claude tells you the price first and makes it in your MegaScenes account when you say yes.

**How the files are made:** each Christmas pet portrait is generated by an image model. Nothing is made until you say yes, and the price in credits comes first.

Limitations: one pet photo per portrait, already in your MegaScenes library; attachments from the chat are not passed to the plugin. Making a file needs a signed-in MegaScenes account and runs in it; nothing is made until you confirm. Not for sexual or adult content or for real people's likeness. Files are made by generative models and saved in your MegaScenes library.

## Install
In Claude Code:
```
/plugin marketplace add megascenes-app/claude-plugins
/plugin install christmas-pet-portrait@megascenes
```

## Connecting
- Christmas Pet Portraits talks to the MegaScenes server at `https://megascenes.com/api/claude/christmas-pet-portrait/mcp` (Streamable HTTP).
- Connecting opens MegaScenes: sign in, create an account, or continue without one and add your email when you pay (OAuth with PKCE). Then you come straight back to the chat.
- Each Christmas pet portrait uses credits from your MegaScenes account and is priced before it runs; new accounts can start with a low-cost trial week.
- The Christmas pet portraits show in a viewer in the chat where Claude supports interactive apps; in Claude Code you get the links.

## Tools
| Tool | What it does |
|---|---|
| `list_christmas_pet_portrait_models` | Models for Christmas pet portraits with settings and starting prices. Read-only. |
| `estimate_christmas_pet_portrait_cost` | Exact price of one Christmas pet portrait before it runs. Read-only. |
| `create_christmas_pet_portrait` | Starts one Christmas pet portrait in your account. Uses credits; needs sign-in. |
| `get_christmas_pet_portrait` | Status and file links. Read-only. |
| `list_my_images` | Your uploads and images made here, to pick the one to start from. Read-only. |
| `get_account` | Your plan and credit balance. Read-only. |

## Example prompts
- "Put a Santa hat on my dog from my latest MegaScenes photo"
- "Make a Christmas portrait of my cat sleeping by the tree"
- "Paint my pet in a knitted holiday sweater"

## Data and privacy
What you give for the Christmas pet portrait (the picture you pick from your library and the details you give) goes to MegaScenes, stays in your MegaScenes account with the finished file, and is passed to the model provider that makes it. The plugin has no hooks or local scripts and sends nothing else.
