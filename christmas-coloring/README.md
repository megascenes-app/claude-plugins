# Christmas Coloring Pages

Create printable Christmas coloring pages with clean black outlines on white, simple for little hands or detailed for adults.

## What it does
Name a Christmas subject and who it is for: a reindeer with a scarf, a gingerbread house, Santa's sleigh over the rooftops, an ornament mandala. You get a coloring page with clean black outlines on white, simple shapes or fine detail. For parents, teachers, holiday activities and anyone who colors to relax. Claude tells you the price first and makes it in your MegaScenes account when you say yes.

**How the files are made:** each Christmas coloring page is generated by an image model. Nothing is made until you say yes, and the price in credits comes first.

Limitations: new pages from a description; it does not turn a photo into a coloring page. Making a file needs a signed-in MegaScenes account and runs in it; nothing is made until you confirm. Not for sexual or adult content or for real people's likeness. Files are made by generative models and saved in your MegaScenes library.

## Install
In Claude Code:
```
/plugin marketplace add megascenes-app/claude-plugins
/plugin install christmas-coloring@megascenes
```

## Connecting
- Christmas Coloring Pages talks to the MegaScenes server at `https://megascenes.com/api/claude/christmas-coloring/mcp` (Streamable HTTP).
- Connecting opens MegaScenes: sign in, create an account, or continue without one and add your email when you pay (OAuth with PKCE). Then you come straight back to the chat.
- Each Christmas coloring page uses credits from your MegaScenes account and is priced before it runs; new accounts can start with a low-cost trial week.
- The Christmas coloring pages show in a viewer in the chat where Claude supports interactive apps; in Claude Code you get the links.

## Tools
| Tool | What it does |
|---|---|
| `list_christmas_coloring_page_models` | Models for Christmas coloring pages with settings and starting prices. Read-only. |
| `estimate_christmas_coloring_page_cost` | Exact price of one Christmas coloring page before it runs. Read-only. |
| `create_christmas_coloring_page` | Starts one Christmas coloring page in your account. Uses credits; needs sign-in. |
| `get_christmas_coloring_page` | Status and file links. Read-only. |
| `get_account` | Your plan and credit balance. Read-only. |

## Example prompts

- "A detailed Christmas ornament mandala coloring page for adults"
- "A coloring page of a gingerbread house with candy canes"

## Data and privacy
What you give for the Christmas coloring page (the details you give) goes to MegaScenes, stays in your MegaScenes account with the finished file, and is passed to the model provider that makes it. The plugin has no hooks or local scripts and sends nothing else.
