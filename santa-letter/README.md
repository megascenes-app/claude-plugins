# Christmas Letter from Santa

Create a printable letter from Santa with the first name and the good deeds you choose, for parents preparing a Christmas surprise.

## What it does
Tell it the first name to write and a few good things from the year: helped at home, learned to ride a bike, was kind to a new friend. You get a festive letter from Santa on North Pole paper, with those words in it, ready to print and leave by the tree. Give only the first name and the details you want in the letter: no address, age or other personal details are needed. For parents and families preparing a Christmas surprise. Claude tells you the price first and makes it in your MegaScenes account when you say yes.

**How the files are made:** each Santa letter is generated by an image model. Nothing is made until you say yes, and the price in credits comes first.

Limitations: an image of the letter to print; your words are passed to the model as written, so check the lettering in the result. Santa is shown as a classic, generic figure. Making a file needs a signed-in MegaScenes account and runs in it; nothing is made until you confirm. Not for sexual or adult content or for real people's likeness. Files are made by generative models and saved in your MegaScenes library.

## Install
In Claude Code:
```
/plugin marketplace add megascenes-app/claude-plugins
/plugin install santa-letter@megascenes
```

## Connecting
- Christmas Letter from Santa talks to the MegaScenes server at `https://megascenes.com/api/claude/santa-letter/mcp` (Streamable HTTP).
- Connecting opens MegaScenes: sign in, create an account, or continue without one and add your email when you pay (OAuth with PKCE). Then you come straight back to the chat.
- Each Santa letter uses credits from your MegaScenes account and is priced before it runs; new accounts can start with a low-cost trial week.
- The Santa letters show in a viewer in the chat where Claude supports interactive apps; in Claude Code you get the links.

## Tools
| Tool | What it does |
|---|---|
| `list_santa_letter_models` | Models for Santa letters with settings and starting prices. Read-only. |
| `estimate_santa_letter_cost` | Exact price of one Santa letter before it runs. Read-only. |
| `create_santa_letter` | Starts one Santa letter in your account. Uses credits; needs sign-in. |
| `get_santa_letter` | Status and file links. Read-only. |
| `get_account` | Your plan and credit balance. Read-only. |

## Example prompts
- "A letter from Santa for Leo, who helped at home and learned to ride a bike this year"
- "A letter from Santa for Mia, thanking her for being kind to her new friend"
- "A letter from Santa for two siblings, Sam and Lucy, who shared their toys"

## Data and privacy
What you give for the Santa letter (the details you give) goes to MegaScenes, stays in your MegaScenes account with the finished file, and is passed to the model provider that makes it. The plugin has no hooks or local scripts and sends nothing else.
