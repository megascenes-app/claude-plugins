# Dog Photoshoot

Give your dog a professional photoshoot from its photo in your MegaScenes library: a studio backdrop, an outfit, a season or a park at golden hour, keeping its breed and markings.

## What it does
Pick a photo of your dog from your MegaScenes library and choose the setting, an outfit and the style: a studio with a pastel backdrop, autumn leaves in a park, a beach at sunset, a cozy sofa, with a bandana, a raincoat or a bow tie, as a studio portrait, candid lifestyle, magazine editorial or black and white. You get a picture that keeps its breed, colors and markings, portrait, square or landscape. For posts, prints, calendars, gifts and your dog's own profile. Claude tells you the price first and makes it in your MegaScenes account when you say yes.

**How the files are made:** each dog photo is generated by an image model. Nothing is made until you say yes, and the price in credits comes first.

Limitations: one dog photo per picture, already in your MegaScenes library; attachments from the chat are not passed to the plugin. Outfits and looks are original, not characters or brands owned by others. Making a file needs a signed-in MegaScenes account and runs in it; nothing is made until you confirm. Not for sexual or adult content or for real people's likeness. Files are made by generative models and saved in your MegaScenes library.

## Install
In Claude Code:
```
/plugin marketplace add megascenes-app/claude-plugins
/plugin install dog-photoshoot@megascenes
```

## Connecting
- Dog Photoshoot talks to the MegaScenes server at `https://megascenes.com/api/claude/dog-photoshoot/mcp` (Streamable HTTP).
- Connecting opens MegaScenes: sign in, create an account, or continue without one and add your email when you pay (OAuth with PKCE). Then you come straight back to the chat.
- Each dog photo uses credits from your MegaScenes account and is priced before it runs; new accounts can start with a low-cost trial week.
- The dog photos show in a viewer in the chat where Claude supports interactive apps; in Claude Code you get the links.

## Tools
| Tool | What it does |
|---|---|
| `list_dog_photoshoot_models` | Models for dog photos with settings and starting prices. Read-only. |
| `estimate_dog_photoshoot_cost` | Exact price of one dog photo before it runs. Read-only. |
| `create_dog_photoshoot` | Starts one dog photo in your account. Uses credits; needs sign-in. |
| `get_dog_photoshoot` | Status and file links. Read-only. |
| `list_my_images` | Your uploads and images made here, to pick the one to start from. Read-only. |
| `get_account` | Your plan and credit balance. Read-only. |

## Example prompts

## Data and privacy
What you give for the dog photo (the picture you pick from your library and the details you give) goes to MegaScenes, stays in your MegaScenes account with the finished file, and is passed to the model provider that makes it. The plugin has no hooks or local scripts and sends nothing else.
