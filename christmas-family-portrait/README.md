# Christmas Family Portrait

Create a Christmas family portrait from your family photos in your MegaScenes library, each person with their own face, by the tree or in the snow.

## What it does
Pick a group photo or one photo per person from your MegaScenes library and choose the Christmas setting: by the tree in matching sweaters, in front of a snowy cabin, around the holiday table, as a painted portrait. You get one picture with everyone together, each with their own face. For holiday cards, prints and family gifts. Claude tells you the price first and makes it in your MegaScenes account when you say yes.

**How the files are made:** each Christmas family portrait is generated by an image model. Nothing is made until you say yes, and the price in credits comes first.

Limitations: only from your own family photos or photos of people who agreed to it; up to six people. The photos must already be in your MegaScenes library; attachments from the chat are not passed to the plugin. Making a file needs a signed-in MegaScenes account and runs in it; nothing is made until you confirm. Not for sexual or adult content or for real people's likeness. Files are made by generative models and saved in your MegaScenes library.

## Install
In Claude Code:
```
/plugin marketplace add megascenes-app/claude-plugins
/plugin install christmas-family-portrait@megascenes
```

## Connecting
- Christmas Family Portrait talks to the MegaScenes server at `https://megascenes.com/api/claude/christmas-family-portrait/mcp` (Streamable HTTP).
- Connecting opens MegaScenes: sign in, create an account, or continue without one and add your email when you pay (OAuth with PKCE). Then you come straight back to the chat.
- Each Christmas family portrait uses credits from your MegaScenes account and is priced before it runs; new accounts can start with a low-cost trial week.
- The Christmas family portraits show in a viewer in the chat where Claude supports interactive apps; in Claude Code you get the links.

## Tools
| Tool | What it does |
|---|---|
| `list_christmas_family_portrait_models` | Models for Christmas family portraits with settings and starting prices. Read-only. |
| `estimate_christmas_family_portrait_cost` | Exact price of one Christmas family portrait before it runs. Read-only. |
| `create_christmas_family_portrait` | Starts one Christmas family portrait in your account. Uses credits; needs sign-in. |
| `get_christmas_family_portrait` | Status and file links. Read-only. |
| `list_my_images` | Your uploads and images made here, to pick the ones to start from. Read-only. |
| `get_account` | Your plan and credit balance. Read-only. |

## Example prompts
- "Make a Christmas family portrait by the tree from my latest MegaScenes photos"
- "Put our family in matching red sweaters in front of a snowy cabin"
- "Create a painted Christmas portrait of my family"

## Data and privacy
What you give for the Christmas family portrait (the pictures you pick from your library and the details you give) goes to MegaScenes, stays in your MegaScenes account with the finished file, and is passed to the model provider that makes it. The plugin has no hooks or local scripts and sends nothing else.
