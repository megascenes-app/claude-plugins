# Thanksgiving Greeting Images

Create Happy Thanksgiving images with your own greeting written in the picture, ready to send in chats and share on social media.

## What it does
Write the greeting you want to share, such as Happy Thanksgiving 2026 or a line of your own, and pick the scene and the look: a harvest table with candles, pumpkins on a porch, golden leaves in the wind, as a cozy photo, watercolor, rustic, a vintage postcard or a cute cartoon. You get a festive image with your words in it, square, tall for stories and status, or wide. For group chats, status updates, stories and Thanksgiving posts. Claude tells you the price first and makes it in your MegaScenes account when you say yes.

**How the files are made:** each Thanksgiving greeting image is generated by an image model. Nothing is made until you say yes, and the price in credits comes first.

Limitations: one image per run; your text is passed to the model as written, so check the lettering in the result. It does not post or send the image for you. Making a file needs a signed-in MegaScenes account and runs in it; nothing is made until you confirm. Not for sexual or adult content or for real people's likeness. Files are made by generative models and saved in your MegaScenes library.

## Install
In Claude Code:
```
/plugin marketplace add megascenes-app/claude-plugins
/plugin install thanksgiving-greeting@megascenes
```

## Connecting
- Thanksgiving Greeting Images talks to the MegaScenes server at `https://megascenes.com/api/claude/thanksgiving-greeting/mcp` (Streamable HTTP).
- Connecting opens MegaScenes: sign in, create an account, or continue without one and add your email when you pay (OAuth with PKCE). Then you come straight back to the chat.
- Each Thanksgiving greeting image uses credits from your MegaScenes account and is priced before it runs; new accounts can start with a low-cost trial week.
- The Thanksgiving greeting images show in a viewer in the chat where Claude supports interactive apps; in Claude Code you get the links.

## Tools
| Tool | What it does |
|---|---|
| `list_thanksgiving_greeting_models` | Models for Thanksgiving greeting images with settings and starting prices. Read-only. |
| `estimate_thanksgiving_greeting_cost` | Exact price of one Thanksgiving greeting image before it runs. Read-only. |
| `create_thanksgiving_greeting` | Starts one Thanksgiving greeting image in your account. Uses credits; needs sign-in. |
| `get_thanksgiving_greeting` | Status and file links. Read-only. |
| `get_account` | Your plan and credit balance. Read-only. |

## Data and privacy
What you give for the Thanksgiving greeting image (the details you give) goes to MegaScenes, stays in your MegaScenes account with the finished file, and is passed to the model provider that makes it. The plugin has no hooks or local scripts and sends nothing else.
