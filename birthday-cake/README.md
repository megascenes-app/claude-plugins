# Birthday Cake Designer

Design a birthday cake as a picture to show your baker or to inspire your own baking: tiers, colors, decorations and the words on the cake.

## What it does
Describe the cake you have in mind and the party theme: a two-tier lemon cake with pressed flowers, a chocolate drip cake with gold balloons, a garden cake, an elegant black and gold cake, with the words you want on top. You get a photo-style picture of the cake to show your baker or to bake from. For bakery orders, home bakers looking for ideas and anyone getting ready for a birthday. Claude tells you the price first and makes it in your MegaScenes account when you say yes.

**How the files are made:** each birthday cake design is generated by an image model. Nothing is made until you say yes, and the price in credits comes first.

Limitations: an inspiration picture, not a recipe, and a baker may make the cake differently. Your text is passed to the model as written, so check the lettering in the result. It does not order or deliver cakes. Making a file needs a signed-in MegaScenes account and runs in it; nothing is made until you confirm. Not for sexual or adult content or for real people's likeness. Files are made by generative models and saved in your MegaScenes library.

## Install
In Claude Code:
```
/plugin marketplace add megascenes-app/claude-plugins
/plugin install birthday-cake@megascenes
```

## Connecting
- Birthday Cake Designer talks to the MegaScenes server at `https://megascenes.com/api/claude/birthday-cake/mcp` (Streamable HTTP).
- Connecting opens MegaScenes: sign in, create an account, or continue without one and add your email when you pay (OAuth with PKCE). Then you come straight back to the chat.
- Each birthday cake design uses credits from your MegaScenes account and is priced before it runs; new accounts can start with a low-cost trial week.
- The birthday cake designs show in a viewer in the chat where Claude supports interactive apps; in Claude Code you get the links.

## Tools
| Tool | What it does |
|---|---|
| `list_birthday_cake_models` | Models for birthday cake designs with settings and starting prices. Read-only. |
| `estimate_birthday_cake_cost` | Exact price of one birthday cake design before it runs. Read-only. |
| `create_birthday_cake` | Starts one birthday cake design in your account. Uses credits; needs sign-in. |
| `get_birthday_cake` | Status and file links. Read-only. |
| `get_account` | Your plan and credit balance. Read-only. |

## Example prompts
- "A two-tier lemon birthday cake with pressed flowers"
- "A chocolate drip cake with gold balloons and the words Happy Birthday Anna"
- "An outer space birthday cake with planets and stars"

## Data and privacy
What you give for the birthday cake design (the details you give) goes to MegaScenes, stays in your MegaScenes account with the finished file, and is passed to the model provider that makes it. The plugin has no hooks or local scripts and sends nothing else.
