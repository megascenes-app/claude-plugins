# Christmas Clipart

Create Christmas clipart and stickers on a clean background: ornaments, snowmen, holly, gifts and more, in the style you choose.

## What it does
Describe the Christmas element and the style: a cute snowman, a sprig of holly, a stack of gifts, a vintage ornament, flat or watercolor. You get the element on a clean background, ready for cards, crafts, labels and designs. For crafts, cards, gift labels, school projects and social posts. Claude tells you the price first and makes it in your MegaScenes account when you say yes.

**How the files are made:** each Christmas clipart image is generated by an image model. Nothing is made until you say yes, and the price in credits comes first.

Limitations: one image per run, on a clean background; original designs only, not characters or logos owned by others. Making a file needs a signed-in MegaScenes account and runs in it; nothing is made until you confirm. Not for sexual or adult content or for real people's likeness. Files are made by generative models and saved in your MegaScenes library.

## Install
In Claude Code:
```
/plugin marketplace add megascenes-app/claude-plugins
/plugin install christmas-clipart@megascenes
```

## Connecting
- Christmas Clipart talks to the MegaScenes server at `https://megascenes.com/api/claude/christmas-clipart/mcp` (Streamable HTTP).
- Connecting opens MegaScenes: sign in, create an account, or continue without one and add your email when you pay (OAuth with PKCE). Then you come straight back to the chat.
- Each Christmas clipart image uses credits from your MegaScenes account and is priced before it runs; new accounts can start with a low-cost trial week.
- The Christmas clipart images show in a viewer in the chat where Claude supports interactive apps; in Claude Code you get the links.

## Tools
| Tool | What it does |
|---|---|
| `list_christmas_clipart_models` | Models for Christmas clipart images with settings and starting prices. Read-only. |
| `estimate_christmas_clipart_cost` | Exact price of one Christmas clipart image before it runs. Read-only. |
| `create_christmas_clipart` | Starts one Christmas clipart image in your account. Uses credits; needs sign-in. |
| `get_christmas_clipart` | Status and file links. Read-only. |
| `get_account` | Your plan and credit balance. Read-only. |

## Example prompts
- "A cute watercolor snowman clipart on a white background"
- "A set of flat red and gold Christmas ornaments as stickers"
- "A sprig of holly with berries, vintage illustration style"

## Data and privacy
What you give for the Christmas clipart image (the details you give) goes to MegaScenes, stays in your MegaScenes account with the finished file, and is passed to the model provider that makes it. The plugin has no hooks or local scripts and sends nothing else.
