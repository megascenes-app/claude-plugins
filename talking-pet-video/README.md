# Talking Pet Videos

Make your pet talk: a short video from a photo of your dog, cat or any animal in your MegaScenes library, saying the words you write in a funny or sweet voice.

## What it does
Pick a photo of your pet from your MegaScenes library, write what it should say, about 15 words, and choose a voice and what it does: a deep grumpy voice, a squeaky excited voice, a posh accent, while it tilts its head, looks guilty or wags its tail. You get a short video with sound of your pet saying your line, wide or tall for phones and stories. For funny posts, birthday messages from the dog, family chats and stories. Claude tells you the price first and makes it in your MegaScenes account when you say yes.

**How the files are made:** each clip is generated by a video model. Nothing is made until you say yes, and the price in credits comes first.

Limitations: animals only, never a photo of a person. A clip of about 8 seconds; the voice and the mouth movement are generated with the video, so watch it before sharing. The photo must already be in your MegaScenes library; attachments from the chat are not passed to the plugin. Making a file needs a signed-in MegaScenes account and runs in it; nothing is made until you confirm. Not for sexual or adult content or for real people's likeness. Files are made by generative models and saved in your MegaScenes library.

## Install
In Claude Code:
```
/plugin marketplace add megascenes-app/claude-plugins
/plugin install talking-pet-video@megascenes
```

## Connecting
- Talking Pet Videos talks to the MegaScenes server at `https://megascenes.com/api/claude/talking-pet-video/mcp` (Streamable HTTP).
- Connecting opens MegaScenes: sign in, create an account, or continue without one and add your email when you pay (OAuth with PKCE). Then you come straight back to the chat.
- Each clip uses credits from your MegaScenes account and is priced before it runs; new accounts can start with a low-cost trial week.
- Clips take one to a few minutes: Claude checks on progress, and the clip plays in a viewer in the chat where Claude supports interactive apps (a link in Claude Code).

## Tools
| Tool | What it does |
|---|---|
| `list_talking_pet_video_models` | Models for talking pet videos with settings and starting prices. Read-only. |
| `estimate_talking_pet_video_cost` | Exact price of one talking pet video before it runs. Read-only. |
| `create_talking_pet_video` | Starts one talking pet video in your account. Uses credits; needs sign-in. |
| `get_talking_pet_video` | Status and file links. Read-only. |
| `list_my_images` | Your uploads and images made here, to pick the one to start from. Read-only. |
| `get_account` | Your plan and credit balance. Read-only. |

## Example prompts
- "Make my dog say: Who ate the cookies? Not me. From my latest MegaScenes photo"
- "My cat complaining about Mondays in a deep grumpy voice"
- "My puppy saying Happy birthday, Anna! in a squeaky excited voice"

## Data and privacy
What you give for the clip (the picture you pick from your library and the details you give) goes to MegaScenes, stays in your MegaScenes account with the finished file, and is passed to the model provider that makes it. The plugin has no hooks or local scripts and sends nothing else.
