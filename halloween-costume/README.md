# Halloween Costume Photos

Try a Halloween look on your own selfie in your MegaScenes library: the same face dressed as a witch, a vampire, a pirate or a friendly ghost, spooky and fun.

## What it does
Pick a selfie or a portrait of yourself from your MegaScenes library and describe the look in general terms: a witch with a pointed hat, a vintage vampire, a pirate captain, a skeleton, a space explorer. Choose the scene and the style if you like: a pumpkin patch at dusk, a haunted mansion, a foggy forest, a party with jack-o'-lanterns, as spooky cinematic, fun and colorful, a vintage movie poster or a moody photo. You get a picture that keeps your face, dressed up the way you described, portrait, square or tall for stories. To pick an outfit before the party, for your Halloween post and profile pictures. Claude tells you the price first and makes it in your MegaScenes account when you say yes.

**How the files are made:** each Halloween costume photo is generated by an image model. Nothing is made until you say yes, and the price in credits comes first.

Limitations: only from a selfie or portrait of yourself, never of a famous person; no swimwear, underwear or sensual shots. Spooky, not gory, and looks described in general terms, not characters or brands owned by others. The photo must already be in your MegaScenes library; attachments from the chat are not passed to the plugin. Making a file needs a signed-in MegaScenes account and runs in it; nothing is made until you confirm. Not for sexual or adult content. Files are made by generative models and saved in your MegaScenes library.

## Install
In Claude Code:
```
/plugin marketplace add megascenes-app/claude-plugins
/plugin install halloween-costume@megascenes
```

## Connecting
- Halloween Costume Photos talks to the MegaScenes server at `https://megascenes.com/api/claude/halloween-costume/mcp` (Streamable HTTP).
- Connecting opens MegaScenes: sign in, create an account, or continue without one and add your email when you pay (OAuth with PKCE). Then you come straight back to the chat.
- Each Halloween costume photo uses credits from your MegaScenes account and is priced before it runs; new accounts can start with a low-cost trial week.
- The Halloween costume photos show in a viewer in the chat where Claude supports interactive apps; in Claude Code you get the links.

## Tools
| Tool | What it does |
|---|---|
| `list_halloween_costume_models` | Models for Halloween costume photos with settings and starting prices. Read-only. |
| `estimate_halloween_costume_cost` | Exact price of one Halloween costume photo before it runs. Read-only. |
| `create_halloween_costume` | Starts one Halloween costume photo in your account. Uses credits; needs sign-in. |
| `get_halloween_costume` | Status and file links. Read-only. |
| `list_my_images` | Your uploads and images made here, to pick the one to start from. Read-only. |
| `get_account` | Your plan and credit balance. Read-only. |

## Example prompts
- "Make a Halloween photo of me as a witch with a pointed hat from my latest MegaScenes selfie"
- "Put me in a haunted mansion as a vintage vampire"
- "Create a fun photo of me as a pirate captain in a pumpkin patch at dusk"

## Data and privacy
What you give for the Halloween costume photo (the picture you pick from your library and the details you give) goes to MegaScenes, stays in your MegaScenes account with the finished file, and is passed to the model provider that makes it. The plugin has no hooks or local scripts and sends nothing else.
