# Birthday Video Maker

Make a short birthday video with music and sound: a festive scene in the party theme you pick, with an optional line spoken or sung in it.

## What it does
Say who the video is for, pick the scene and, if you like, add a short line to be spoken or sung: a cake with glowing candles and falling confetti, a garden party at sunset, a disco with neon lights, an underwater adventure. You get a short festive clip with music and party sounds, wide for screens or tall for phones and stories. Give only a first name and the words to say: no address or other personal details are needed. For birthday messages, group chats, stories and the screen at the party. Claude tells you the price first and makes it in your MegaScenes account when you say yes.

**How the files are made:** each clip is generated by a video model. Nothing is made until you say yes, and the price in credits comes first.

Limitations: a short clip of a few seconds, made from your description, not from your photos. The sound is made with the video and a spoken line of about 15 words follows your words as closely as the model can, so watch it before sharing. Party themes are original, not characters or brands owned by others. Making a file needs a signed-in MegaScenes account and runs in it; nothing is made until you confirm. Not for sexual or adult content or for real people's likeness. Files are made by generative models and saved in your MegaScenes library.

## Install
In Claude Code:
```
/plugin marketplace add megascenes-app/claude-plugins
/plugin install birthday-video@megascenes
```

## Connecting
- Birthday Video Maker talks to the MegaScenes server at `https://megascenes.com/api/claude/birthday-video/mcp` (Streamable HTTP).
- Connecting opens MegaScenes: sign in, create an account, or continue without one and add your email when you pay (OAuth with PKCE). Then you come straight back to the chat.
- Each clip uses credits from your MegaScenes account and is priced before it runs; new accounts can start with a low-cost trial week.
- Clips take one to a few minutes: Claude checks on progress, and the clip plays in a viewer in the chat where Claude supports interactive apps (a link in Claude Code).

## Tools
| Tool | What it does |
|---|---|
| `list_birthday_video_models` | Models for birthday videos with settings and starting prices. Read-only. |
| `estimate_birthday_video_cost` | Exact price of one birthday video before it runs. Read-only. |
| `create_birthday_video` | Starts one birthday video in your account. Uses credits; needs sign-in. |
| `get_birthday_video` | Status and file links. Read-only. |
| `get_account` | Your plan and credit balance. Read-only. |

## Example prompts
- "A birthday video for Anna with a cake, glowing candles, balloons and confetti"
- "A neon disco birthday video where a voice says Happy birthday, Sam!"
- "A tall birthday video of a garden party at sunset for my stories"

## Data and privacy
What you give for the clip (the details you give) goes to MegaScenes, stays in your MegaScenes account with the finished file, and is passed to the model provider that makes it. The plugin has no hooks or local scripts and sends nothing else.
