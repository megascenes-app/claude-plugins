# Love Song Maker

Create an original love song with vocals for the person you love: their name, your story and what you love about them, in the style you choose.

## What it does
Give the name to sing, a few lines about your story and what you love about them, and the style: R&B, acoustic, a pop ballad, soul, country, indie, with a female or male lead or a duet. You get an original love song with vocals as an audio file, with lyrics written from your story or the lyrics you give. The voice sings the name you give. Give only the name and the details you want sung: no address or contact details are needed. For Valentine's Day, a proposal, an anniversary or just because. Claude tells you the price first and makes it in your MegaScenes account when you say yes.

**How the files are made:** each love song is generated by an audio model. Nothing is made until you say yes, and the price in credits comes first.

Limitations: original songs; it does not imitate a real singer's voice or copy an existing song or artist. Making a file needs a signed-in MegaScenes account and runs in it; nothing is made until you confirm. Not for sexual or adult content or for real people's likeness. Files are made by generative models and saved in your MegaScenes library.

## Install
In Claude Code:
```
/plugin marketplace add megascenes-app/claude-plugins
/plugin install love-song@megascenes
```

## Connecting
- Love Song Maker talks to the MegaScenes server at `https://megascenes.com/api/claude/love-song/mcp` (Streamable HTTP).
- Connecting opens MegaScenes: sign in, create an account, or continue without one and add your email when you pay (OAuth with PKCE). Then you come straight back to the chat.
- Each love song uses credits from your MegaScenes account and is priced before it runs; new accounts can start with a low-cost trial week.
- The love song plays in a viewer in the chat where Claude supports interactive apps; in Claude Code you get the link.

## Tools
| Tool | What it does |
|---|---|
| `list_love_song_models` | Models for love songs with settings and starting prices. Read-only. |
| `estimate_love_song_cost` | Exact price of one love song before it runs. Read-only. |
| `create_love_song` | Starts one love song in your account. Uses credits; needs sign-in. |
| `get_love_song` | Status and file links. Read-only. |
| `get_account` | Your plan and credit balance. Read-only. |

## Example prompts
- "An R&B love song for Sofia, who I met at a concert"
- "An acoustic love song about her laugh and our Sunday mornings"
- "A pop ballad for a proposal, sung by a male voice"

## Data and privacy
What you give for the love song (the details you give) goes to MegaScenes, stays in your MegaScenes account with the finished file, and is passed to the model provider that makes it. The plugin has no hooks or local scripts and sends nothing else.
