# Easter Coloring Pages

Create printable Easter coloring pages with clean black outlines on white: bunnies, painted eggs, chicks, spring flowers and baskets, simple or detailed.

## What it does
Name an Easter subject and how detailed it should be: a bunny with a basket of eggs, an egg mandala, chicks in a spring meadow, a lamb among daisies, in simple bold shapes, medium detail or intricate patterns. You get a coloring page with clean black outlines on white. For parents, teachers and anyone who loves to color, for a class activity, an Easter morning or a quiet afternoon. Claude tells you the price first and makes it in your MegaScenes account when you say yes.

**How the files are made:** each Easter coloring page is generated by an image model. Nothing is made until you say yes, and the price in credits comes first.

Limitations: new pages from a description, without text on the page; it does not turn a photo into a coloring page. Designs are original, not characters or brands owned by others. Making a file needs a signed-in MegaScenes account and runs in it; nothing is made until you confirm. Not for sexual or adult content or for real people's likeness. Files are made by generative models and saved in your MegaScenes library.

## Install
In Claude Code:
```
/plugin marketplace add megascenes-app/claude-plugins
/plugin install easter-coloring@megascenes
```

## Connecting
- Easter Coloring Pages talks to the MegaScenes server at `https://megascenes.com/api/claude/easter-coloring/mcp` (Streamable HTTP).
- Connecting opens MegaScenes: sign in, create an account, or continue without one and add your email when you pay (OAuth with PKCE). Then you come straight back to the chat.
- Each Easter coloring page uses credits from your MegaScenes account and is priced before it runs; new accounts can start with a low-cost trial week.
- The Easter coloring pages show in a viewer in the chat where Claude supports interactive apps; in Claude Code you get the links.

## Tools
| Tool | What it does |
|---|---|
| `list_easter_coloring_page_models` | Models for Easter coloring pages with settings and starting prices. Read-only. |
| `estimate_easter_coloring_page_cost` | Exact price of one Easter coloring page before it runs. Read-only. |
| `create_easter_coloring_page` | Starts one Easter coloring page in your account. Uses credits; needs sign-in. |
| `get_easter_coloring_page` | Status and file links. Read-only. |
| `get_account` | Your plan and credit balance. Read-only. |

## Example prompts

- "A detailed Easter egg mandala coloring page for adults"
- "A coloring page of chicks in a spring meadow"

## Data and privacy
What you give for the Easter coloring page (the details you give) goes to MegaScenes, stays in your MegaScenes account with the finished file, and is passed to the model provider that makes it. The plugin has no hooks or local scripts and sends nothing else.
