# Mother's Day Song Maker

Create an original Mother's Day song with vocals for your mom or grandma: her name, your memories together and what she means to you.

## What it does
Give the name to sing, a few memories to sing about and the style: an acoustic ballad, pop, soul, country or folk, with a female or male lead, a duet or a choir. You get an original Mother's Day song with vocals as an audio file, with lyrics written from your memories or the lyrics you give. The voice sings the name you give. Give only the name and the details you want sung: no address or contact details are needed. To play at a Mother's Day lunch, send in the family chat or add to a video for her. Claude tells you the price first and makes it in your MegaScenes account when you say yes.

**How the files are made:** each Mother's Day song is generated by an audio model. Nothing is made until you say yes, and the price in credits comes first.

Limitations: original songs; it does not imitate a real singer's voice or copy an existing song or artist. Making a file needs a signed-in MegaScenes account and runs in it; nothing is made until you confirm. Not for sexual or adult content or for real people's likeness. Files are made by generative models and saved in your MegaScenes library.

## Install
In Claude Code:
```
/plugin marketplace add megascenes-app/claude-plugins
/plugin install mothers-day-song@megascenes
```

## Connecting
- Mother's Day Song Maker talks to the MegaScenes server at `https://megascenes.com/api/claude/mothers-day-song/mcp` (Streamable HTTP).
- Connecting opens MegaScenes: sign in, create an account, or continue without one and add your email when you pay (OAuth with PKCE). Then you come straight back to the chat.
- Each Mother's Day song uses credits from your MegaScenes account and is priced before it runs; new accounts can start with a low-cost trial week.
- The Mother's Day song plays in a viewer in the chat where Claude supports interactive apps; in Claude Code you get the link.

## Tools
| Tool | What it does |
|---|---|
| `list_mothers_day_song_models` | Models for Mother's Day songs with settings and starting prices. Read-only. |
| `estimate_mothers_day_song_cost` | Exact price of one Mother's Day song before it runs. Read-only. |
| `create_mothers_day_song` | Starts one Mother's Day song in your account. Uses credits; needs sign-in. |
| `get_mothers_day_song` | Status and file links. Read-only. |
| `get_account` | Your plan and credit balance. Read-only. |

## Example prompts
- "An acoustic ballad for Mom about Sunday pancakes and her garden"
- "A soul song for Mama Rosa about the songs she sang to us"
- "A funny pop song for my mom about her endless voice messages"

## Data and privacy
What you give for the Mother's Day song (the details you give) goes to MegaScenes, stays in your MegaScenes account with the finished file, and is passed to the model provider that makes it. The plugin has no hooks or local scripts and sends nothing else.
