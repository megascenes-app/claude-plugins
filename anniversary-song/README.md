# Anniversary Song Maker

Create an original anniversary song with vocals about the two of you: your names, the years together and the moments you shared, in the style you choose.

## What it does
Give the names to sing, the years you are celebrating, a few moments you shared and the style: a romantic ballad, acoustic, soul, country, jazz, pop, with a female or male lead or a duet. You get an original anniversary song with vocals as an audio file, with lyrics written from your names and memories or the lyrics you give. The voice sings the names you give. For a gift to your partner, your parents' milestone anniversary, a toast or a video message. Claude tells you the price first and makes it in your MegaScenes account when you say yes.

**How the files are made:** each anniversary song is generated by an audio model. Nothing is made until you say yes, and the price in credits comes first.

Limitations: original songs; it does not imitate a real singer's voice or copy an existing song or artist. Making a file needs a signed-in MegaScenes account and runs in it; nothing is made until you confirm. Not for sexual or adult content or for real people's likeness. Files are made by generative models and saved in your MegaScenes library.

## Install
In Claude Code:
```
/plugin marketplace add megascenes-app/claude-plugins
/plugin install anniversary-song@megascenes
```

## Connecting
- Anniversary Song Maker talks to the MegaScenes server at `https://megascenes.com/api/claude/anniversary-song/mcp` (Streamable HTTP).
- Connecting opens MegaScenes: sign in, create an account, or continue without one and add your email when you pay (OAuth with PKCE). Then you come straight back to the chat.
- Each anniversary song uses credits from your MegaScenes account and is priced before it runs; new accounts can start with a low-cost trial week.
- The anniversary song plays in a viewer in the chat where Claude supports interactive apps; in Claude Code you get the link.

## Tools
| Tool | What it does |
|---|---|
| `list_anniversary_song_models` | Models for anniversary songs with settings and starting prices. Read-only. |
| `estimate_anniversary_song_cost` | Exact price of one anniversary song before it runs. Read-only. |
| `create_anniversary_song` | Starts one anniversary song in your account. Uses credits; needs sign-in. |
| `get_anniversary_song` | Status and file links. Read-only. |
| `get_account` | Your plan and credit balance. Read-only. |

## Example prompts
- "A romantic ballad for Giulia and Marco's 10th anniversary about their first apartment"
- "A jazz duet for my parents' 40th anniversary"
- "An acoustic anniversary song about our trip to Japan and Sunday dinners"

## Data and privacy
What you give for the anniversary song (the details you give) goes to MegaScenes, stays in your MegaScenes account with the finished file, and is passed to the model provider that makes it. The plugin has no hooks or local scripts and sends nothing else.
