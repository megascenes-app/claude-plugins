# Bedtime Story Maker

Turn a bedtime story into a recording read by a calm, warm voice, to play at night: a story you write together with Claude or one of your own.

## What it does
Write a gentle story with Claude, a few minutes long, with your little one's name and favorite things in it, or paste a story you wrote. Pick a calm voice. You get the story read aloud as an audio file, up to about 6 minutes, to play at bedtime. For parents, grandparents and teachers, for bedtime, car trips and quiet time. Claude tells you the price first and makes it in your MegaScenes account when you say yes.

**How the files are made:** each bedtime story is generated by an audio model. Nothing is made until you say yes, and the price in credits comes first.

Limitations: a reading of the text you give, up to 5,000 characters, about 6 minutes; original stories only, not characters or brands owned by others. Music and sound effects are not added. Making a file needs a signed-in MegaScenes account and runs in it; nothing is made until you confirm. Not for sexual or adult content or for real people's likeness. Files are made by generative models and saved in your MegaScenes library.

## Install
In Claude Code:
```
/plugin marketplace add megascenes-app/claude-plugins
/plugin install bedtime-story@megascenes
```

## Connecting
- Bedtime Story Maker talks to the MegaScenes server at `https://megascenes.com/api/claude/bedtime-story/mcp` (Streamable HTTP).
- Connecting opens MegaScenes: sign in, create an account, or continue without one and add your email when you pay (OAuth with PKCE). Then you come straight back to the chat.
- Each bedtime story uses credits from your MegaScenes account and is priced before it runs; new accounts can start with a low-cost trial week.
- The bedtime story plays in a viewer in the chat where Claude supports interactive apps; in Claude Code you get the link.

## Tools
| Tool | What it does |
|---|---|
| `list_bedtime_story_models` | Models for bedtime stories with settings and starting prices. Read-only. |
| `estimate_bedtime_story_cost` | Exact price of one bedtime story before it runs. Read-only. |
| `create_bedtime_story` | Starts one bedtime story in your account. Uses credits; needs sign-in. |
| `get_bedtime_story` | Status and file links. Read-only. |
| `get_account` | Your plan and credit balance. Read-only. |

## Example prompts
- "Write a gentle bedtime story about Leo and a sleepy owl, then read it aloud"
- "Read my bedtime story aloud in a calm, warm voice"
- "A five-minute story about a little cloud who can't fall asleep, read aloud"

## Data and privacy
What you give for the bedtime story (the details you give) goes to MegaScenes, stays in your MegaScenes account with the finished file, and is passed to the model provider that makes it. The plugin has no hooks or local scripts and sends nothing else.
