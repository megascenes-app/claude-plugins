# Christmas Photoshoot

Create Christmas photos of yourself from your own photo in your MegaScenes library: the same face by the tree, in the snow or in a cozy sweater.

## What it does
Pick a selfie or a portrait of yourself from your MegaScenes library and choose the Christmas scene: by a glowing tree, in a snowy street, in a red sweater by the fireplace, in a winter market. You get a picture that keeps your face, in the scene and outfit you chose. For holiday cards, profile pictures and Christmas posts. Claude tells you the price first and makes it in your MegaScenes account when you say yes.

**How the files are made:** each Christmas photo is generated by an image model. Nothing is made until you say yes, and the price in credits comes first.

Limitations: only from your own photo or one of someone who agreed to it, never of a famous person. The photo must already be in your MegaScenes library; attachments from the chat are not passed to the plugin. Making a file needs a signed-in MegaScenes account and runs in it; nothing is made until you confirm. Not for sexual or adult content or for real people's likeness. Files are made by generative models and saved in your MegaScenes library.

## Install
In Claude Code:
```
/plugin marketplace add megascenes-app/claude-plugins
/plugin install christmas-photoshoot@megascenes
```

## Connecting
- Christmas Photoshoot talks to the MegaScenes server at `https://megascenes.com/api/claude/christmas-photoshoot/mcp` (Streamable HTTP).
- Connecting opens MegaScenes: sign in, create an account, or continue without one and add your email when you pay (OAuth with PKCE). Then you come straight back to the chat.
- Each Christmas photo uses credits from your MegaScenes account and is priced before it runs; new accounts can start with a low-cost trial week.
- The Christmas photos show in a viewer in the chat where Claude supports interactive apps; in Claude Code you get the links.

## Tools
| Tool | What it does |
|---|---|
| `list_christmas_photoshoot_models` | Models for Christmas photos with settings and starting prices. Read-only. |
| `estimate_christmas_photoshoot_cost` | Exact price of one Christmas photo before it runs. Read-only. |
| `create_christmas_photoshoot` | Starts one Christmas photo in your account. Uses credits; needs sign-in. |
| `get_christmas_photoshoot` | Status and file links. Read-only. |
| `list_my_images` | Your uploads and images made here, to pick the one to start from. Read-only. |
| `get_account` | Your plan and credit balance. Read-only. |

## Example prompts
- "Make a Christmas photo of me by a glowing tree from my latest MegaScenes selfie"
- "Put me in a snowy street in a red coat"
- "Create a cozy Christmas portrait of me in a sweater by the fireplace"

## Data and privacy
What you give for the Christmas photo (the picture you pick from your library and the details you give) goes to MegaScenes, stays in your MegaScenes account with the finished file, and is passed to the model provider that makes it. The plugin has no hooks or local scripts and sends nothing else.
