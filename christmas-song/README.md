# Christmas Song

Create an original Christmas song with vocals about your family, your names and your memories, in the style you choose.

## What it does
Give the names to sing, a few memories of the year and the style: jolly pop with sleigh bells, a soft acoustic carol, a swing big band, a funny song for the family table. You get an original Christmas song with vocals as an audio file. For family gatherings, holiday videos, gifts and office parties. Claude tells you the price first and makes it in your MegaScenes account when you say yes.

**How the files are made:** each Christmas song is generated by an audio model. Nothing is made until you say yes, and the price in credits comes first.

Limitations: original songs; it does not imitate a real singer's voice or copy an existing carol or hit. Making a file needs a signed-in MegaScenes account and runs in it; nothing is made until you confirm. Not for sexual or adult content or for real people's likeness. Files are made by generative models and saved in your MegaScenes library.

## Install
In Claude Code:
```
/plugin marketplace add megascenes-app/claude-plugins
/plugin install christmas-song@megascenes
```

## Connecting
- Christmas Song talks to the MegaScenes server at `https://megascenes.com/api/claude/christmas-song/mcp` (Streamable HTTP).
- Connecting opens MegaScenes: sign in, create an account, or continue without one and add your email when you pay (OAuth with PKCE). Then you come straight back to the chat.
- Each Christmas song uses credits from your MegaScenes account and is priced before it runs; new accounts can start with a low-cost trial week.
- The Christmas song plays in a viewer in the chat where Claude supports interactive apps; in Claude Code you get the link.

## Tools
| Tool | What it does |
|---|---|
| `list_christmas_song_models` | Models for Christmas songs with settings and starting prices. Read-only. |
| `estimate_christmas_song_cost` | Exact price of one Christmas song before it runs. Read-only. |
| `create_christmas_song` | Starts one Christmas song in your account. Uses credits; needs sign-in. |
| `get_christmas_song` | Status and file links. Read-only. |
| `get_account` | Your plan and credit balance. Read-only. |

## Example prompts
- "A jolly Christmas pop song with sleigh bells for the Rossi family"
- "A soft acoustic Christmas carol about our first Christmas in the new house"
- "A funny Christmas song for my team about this year at the office"

## Data and privacy
What you give for the Christmas song (the details you give) goes to MegaScenes, stays in your MegaScenes account with the finished file, and is passed to the model provider that makes it. The plugin has no hooks or local scripts and sends nothing else.
