# New Year Video Maker

Make a short Happy New Year video with music and sound to send to the people you care about: fireworks, a countdown, confetti and an optional line spoken in the video.

## What it does
Describe the scene and who it is for: fireworks over the sea, a snowy village at midnight, a neon countdown in the city, confetti falling over a rooftop party, for the whole family, your friends or your team. Add a short line to be spoken in the video if you like, about 15 words, such as Happy New Year 2027! You get a short video with music and sound, wide or tall for phones and stories. For group chats, stories, family messages and greetings to clients and colleagues. Claude tells you the price first and makes it in your MegaScenes account when you say yes.

**How the files are made:** each clip is generated by a video model. Nothing is made until you say yes, and the price in credits comes first.

Limitations: a short clip of a few seconds; the music, the sound and the spoken line are generated with the video, so watch it before sharing. It does not post or send the video for you. Making a file needs a signed-in MegaScenes account and runs in it; nothing is made until you confirm. Not for sexual or adult content or for real people's likeness. Files are made by generative models and saved in your MegaScenes library.

## Install
In Claude Code:
```
/plugin marketplace add megascenes-app/claude-plugins
/plugin install new-year-video@megascenes
```

## Connecting
- New Year Video Maker talks to the MegaScenes server at `https://megascenes.com/api/claude/new-year-video/mcp` (Streamable HTTP).
- Connecting opens MegaScenes: sign in, create an account, or continue without one and add your email when you pay (OAuth with PKCE). Then you come straight back to the chat.
- Each clip uses credits from your MegaScenes account and is priced before it runs; new accounts can start with a low-cost trial week.
- Clips take one to a few minutes: Claude checks on progress, and the clip plays in a viewer in the chat where Claude supports interactive apps (a link in Claude Code).

## Tools
| Tool | What it does |
|---|---|
| `list_new_year_video_models` | Models for New Year videos with settings and starting prices. Read-only. |
| `estimate_new_year_video_cost` | Exact price of one New Year video before it runs. Read-only. |
| `create_new_year_video` | Starts one New Year video in your account. Uses credits; needs sign-in. |
| `get_new_year_video` | Status and file links. Read-only. |
| `get_account` | Your plan and credit balance. Read-only. |

## Example prompts
- "A Happy New Year video with fireworks over the sea for the whole family"
- "A neon countdown in the city that ends with Happy New Year 2027!"
- "A snowy village at midnight with fireworks, tall for stories"

## Data and privacy
What you give for the clip (the details you give) goes to MegaScenes, stays in your MegaScenes account with the finished file, and is passed to the model provider that makes it. The plugin has no hooks or local scripts and sends nothing else.
