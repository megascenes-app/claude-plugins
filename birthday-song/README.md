# Birthday Song Maker

Create an original birthday song with vocals that sings the name of the person you celebrate, with your memories and inside jokes, in the style you choose.

## What it does
Give the name to sing, a few memories or inside jokes and the style: a pop anthem, an acoustic ballad, country, R&B, jazz. Pick a female or male lead, a duet or a choir. You get an original birthday song with vocals as an audio file, with lyrics written from your details or the lyrics you give. Give only the name and the details you want sung: no address or contact details are needed. For birthday surprises, parties, video messages and gifts. Claude tells you the price first and makes it in your MegaScenes account when you say yes.

**How the files are made:** each birthday song is generated by an audio model. Nothing is made until you say yes, and the price in credits comes first.

Limitations: original songs; it does not imitate a real singer's voice or copy an existing song or artist. Making a file needs a signed-in MegaScenes account and runs in it; nothing is made until you confirm. Not for sexual or adult content or for real people's likeness. Files are made by generative models and saved in your MegaScenes library.

## Install
In Claude Code:
```
/plugin marketplace add megascenes-app/claude-plugins
/plugin install birthday-song@megascenes
```

## Connecting
- Birthday Song Maker talks to the MegaScenes server at `https://megascenes.com/api/claude/birthday-song/mcp` (Streamable HTTP).
- Connecting opens MegaScenes: sign in, create an account, or continue without one and add your email when you pay (OAuth with PKCE). Then you come straight back to the chat.
- Each birthday song uses credits from your MegaScenes account and is priced before it runs; new accounts can start with a low-cost trial week.
- The birthday song plays in a viewer in the chat where Claude supports interactive apps; in Claude Code you get the link.

## Tools
| Tool | What it does |
|---|---|
| `list_birthday_song_models` | Models for birthday songs with settings and starting prices. Read-only. |
| `estimate_birthday_song_cost` | Exact price of one birthday song before it runs. Read-only. |
| `create_birthday_song` | Starts one birthday song in your account. Uses credits; needs sign-in. |
| `get_birthday_song` | Status and file links. Read-only. |
| `get_account` | Your plan and credit balance. Read-only. |

## Example prompts
- "A pop birthday song for Anna, who loves surfing and is always late"
- "An acoustic birthday ballad for my dad with a male voice"
- "A funny country birthday song for Sam about our trip to Lisbon"

## Data and privacy
What you give for the birthday song (the details you give) goes to MegaScenes, stays in your MegaScenes account with the finished file, and is passed to the model provider that makes it. The plugin has no hooks or local scripts and sends nothing else.
