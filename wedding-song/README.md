# Wedding Song Maker

Create an original wedding song with vocals about the couple: their names, how they met and their story, in the style you choose.

## What it does
Give the couple's names, how they met and a few moments to sing about, and the style: a romantic ballad, acoustic, soul, country, jazz, pop, with a female or male lead or a duet. You get an original wedding song with vocals as an audio file, with lyrics written from your story or the lyrics you give. For the first dance, the entrance, a toast, an anniversary or a gift for the couple. Claude tells you the price first and makes it in your MegaScenes account when you say yes.

**How the files are made:** each wedding song is generated by an audio model. Nothing is made until you say yes, and the price in credits comes first.

Limitations: original songs; it does not imitate a real singer's voice or copy an existing song or artist. Making a file needs a signed-in MegaScenes account and runs in it; nothing is made until you confirm. Not for sexual or adult content or for real people's likeness. Files are made by generative models and saved in your MegaScenes library.

## Install
In Claude Code:
```
/plugin marketplace add megascenes-app/claude-plugins
/plugin install wedding-song@megascenes
```

## Connecting
- Wedding Song Maker talks to the MegaScenes server at `https://megascenes.com/api/claude/wedding-song/mcp` (Streamable HTTP).
- Connecting opens MegaScenes: sign in, create an account, or continue without one and add your email when you pay (OAuth with PKCE). Then you come straight back to the chat.
- Each wedding song uses credits from your MegaScenes account and is priced before it runs; new accounts can start with a low-cost trial week.
- The wedding song plays in a viewer in the chat where Claude supports interactive apps; in Claude Code you get the link.

## Tools
| Tool | What it does |
|---|---|
| `list_wedding_song_models` | Models for wedding songs with settings and starting prices. Read-only. |
| `estimate_wedding_song_cost` | Exact price of one wedding song before it runs. Read-only. |
| `create_wedding_song` | Starts one wedding song in your account. Uses credits; needs sign-in. |
| `get_wedding_song` | Status and file links. Read-only. |
| `get_account` | Your plan and credit balance. Read-only. |

## Example prompts
- "A romantic ballad for Giulia and Marco, who met on a train to Rome"
- "An upbeat soul song for our wedding entrance about the dog we adopted"
- "An acoustic duet for our first dance"

## Data and privacy
What you give for the wedding song (the details you give) goes to MegaScenes, stays in your MegaScenes account with the finished file, and is passed to the model provider that makes it. The plugin has no hooks or local scripts and sends nothing else.
