# Anniversary Greeting Images

Create Happy Anniversary images with your own greeting written in the picture, to send to your partner, your parents or friends.

## What it does
Write the greeting you want to send, such as Happy 10th Anniversary or a line of your own, and pick the scene and the look: red roses and candles, two glasses of champagne, a beach at sunset, in elegant gold, watercolor, photo, vintage or cute cartoon. You get a romantic image with your words in it, square, tall for stories and status, or wide. For messages to your partner, wishes to your parents or friends, stories and anniversary posts. Claude tells you the price first and makes it in your MegaScenes account when you say yes.

**How the files are made:** each anniversary greeting image is generated by an image model. Nothing is made until you say yes, and the price in credits comes first.

Limitations: one image per run; your text is passed to the model as written, so check the lettering in the result. It does not post or send the image for you. Making a file needs a signed-in MegaScenes account and runs in it; nothing is made until you confirm. Not for sexual or adult content or for real people's likeness. Files are made by generative models and saved in your MegaScenes library.

## Install
In Claude Code:
```
/plugin marketplace add megascenes-app/claude-plugins
/plugin install anniversary-greeting@megascenes
```

## Connecting
- Anniversary Greeting Images talks to the MegaScenes server at `https://megascenes.com/api/claude/anniversary-greeting/mcp` (Streamable HTTP).
- Connecting opens MegaScenes: sign in, create an account, or continue without one and add your email when you pay (OAuth with PKCE). Then you come straight back to the chat.
- Each anniversary greeting image uses credits from your MegaScenes account and is priced before it runs; new accounts can start with a low-cost trial week.
- The anniversary greeting images show in a viewer in the chat where Claude supports interactive apps; in Claude Code you get the links.

## Tools
| Tool | What it does |
|---|---|
| `list_anniversary_greeting_models` | Models for anniversary greeting images with settings and starting prices. Read-only. |
| `estimate_anniversary_greeting_cost` | Exact price of one anniversary greeting image before it runs. Read-only. |
| `create_anniversary_greeting` | Starts one anniversary greeting image in your account. Uses credits; needs sign-in. |
| `get_anniversary_greeting` | Status and file links. Read-only. |
| `get_account` | Your plan and credit balance. Read-only. |

## Example prompts
- "A Happy Anniversary image with red roses and glowing candles"
- "A Happy 25th Anniversary image in elegant gold for my parents"
- "A beach at sunset with the words Happy Anniversary, my love, tall for my status"

## Data and privacy
What you give for the anniversary greeting image (the details you give) goes to MegaScenes, stays in your MegaScenes account with the finished file, and is passed to the model provider that makes it. The plugin has no hooks or local scripts and sends nothing else.
