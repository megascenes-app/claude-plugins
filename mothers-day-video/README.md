# Mother's Day Video Maker

Make a short Mother's Day video with gentle music for your mom: a tender scene of flowers, a garden or a sunrise, with an optional line spoken in the video.

## What it does
Describe the scene and who it is for: a field of tulips at sunrise, a breakfast tray with flowers, a garden in bloom, a bouquet in soft morning light by a window, for your mom, your grandma or every mother in the family. Add a short line to be spoken in the video if you like, about 15 words, such as Happy Mother's Day, Mom! You get a short video with gentle music and sound, wide or tall for phones and stories. For family chats, stories and a message for her on Mother's Day. Claude tells you the price first and makes it in your MegaScenes account when you say yes.

**How the files are made:** each clip is generated by a video model. Nothing is made until you say yes, and the price in credits comes first.

Limitations: a short clip of a few seconds, made from your description, not from your photos; the music, the sound and the spoken line are generated with the video, so watch it before sharing. It does not post or send the video for you. Making a file needs a signed-in MegaScenes account and runs in it; nothing is made until you confirm. Not for sexual or adult content or for real people's likeness. Files are made by generative models and saved in your MegaScenes library.

## Install
In Claude Code:
```
/plugin marketplace add megascenes-app/claude-plugins
/plugin install mothers-day-video@megascenes
```

## Connecting
- Mother's Day Video Maker talks to the MegaScenes server at `https://megascenes.com/api/claude/mothers-day-video/mcp` (Streamable HTTP).
- Connecting opens MegaScenes: sign in, create an account, or continue without one and add your email when you pay (OAuth with PKCE). Then you come straight back to the chat.
- Each clip uses credits from your MegaScenes account and is priced before it runs; new accounts can start with a low-cost trial week.
- Clips take one to a few minutes: Claude checks on progress, and the clip plays in a viewer in the chat where Claude supports interactive apps (a link in Claude Code).

## Tools
| Tool | What it does |
|---|---|
| `list_mothers_day_video_models` | Models for Mother's Day videos with settings and starting prices. Read-only. |
| `estimate_mothers_day_video_cost` | Exact price of one Mother's Day video before it runs. Read-only. |
| `create_mothers_day_video` | Starts one Mother's Day video in your account. Uses credits; needs sign-in. |
| `get_mothers_day_video` | Status and file links. Read-only. |
| `get_account` | Your plan and credit balance. Read-only. |

## Example prompts
- "A Mother's Day video of a field of tulips at sunrise for my mom"
- "A breakfast tray with flowers and a voice saying Happy Mother's Day, Mom!"
- "A garden in bloom for Grandma Rosa, tall for stories"

## Data and privacy
What you give for the clip (the details you give) goes to MegaScenes, stays in your MegaScenes account with the finished file, and is passed to the model provider that makes it. The plugin has no hooks or local scripts and sends nothing else.
