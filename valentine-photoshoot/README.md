# Valentine Photoshoot

Create Valentine's Day photos from your own photos in your MegaScenes library, of yourself or of the two of you: the same faces with roses and candlelight, on a rooftop at sunset or in Paris at night.

## What it does
Pick a photo of yourself, a photo of the two of you or one photo of each from your MegaScenes library, and choose the scene, the outfits and the style: red roses and candlelight, a rooftop at sunset, Paris at night, a field of tulips, in a red dress, a black suit or cozy matching sweaters, romantic editorial, candid film, black and white or dreamy soft focus. You get a picture that keeps the same faces, in the scene you chose, portrait, square or tall for stories. For Valentine's posts, surprises, gifts and profile pictures. Claude tells you the price first and makes it in your MegaScenes account when you say yes.

**How the files are made:** each Valentine photo is generated by an image model. Nothing is made until you say yes, and the price in credits comes first.

Limitations: only you or the couple from your own photos, with the consent of both, never a famous person; no swimwear, underwear or sensual shots. One or two photos, already in your MegaScenes library; attachments from the chat are not passed to the plugin. Making a file needs a signed-in MegaScenes account and runs in it; nothing is made until you confirm. Not for sexual or adult content. Files are made by generative models and saved in your MegaScenes library.

## Install
In Claude Code:
```
/plugin marketplace add megascenes-app/claude-plugins
/plugin install valentine-photoshoot@megascenes
```

## Connecting
- Valentine Photoshoot talks to the MegaScenes server at `https://megascenes.com/api/claude/valentine-photoshoot/mcp` (Streamable HTTP).
- Connecting opens MegaScenes: sign in, create an account, or continue without one and add your email when you pay (OAuth with PKCE). Then you come straight back to the chat.
- Each Valentine photo uses credits from your MegaScenes account and is priced before it runs; new accounts can start with a low-cost trial week.
- The Valentine photos show in a viewer in the chat where Claude supports interactive apps; in Claude Code you get the links.

## Tools
| Tool | What it does |
|---|---|
| `list_valentine_photoshoot_models` | Models for Valentine photos with settings and starting prices. Read-only. |
| `estimate_valentine_photoshoot_cost` | Exact price of one Valentine photo before it runs. Read-only. |
| `create_valentine_photoshoot` | Starts one Valentine photo in your account. Uses credits; needs sign-in. |
| `get_valentine_photoshoot` | Status and file links. Read-only. |
| `list_my_images` | Your uploads and images made here, to pick the ones to start from. Read-only. |
| `get_account` | Your plan and credit balance. Read-only. |

## Example prompts
- "Make a Valentine's photo of us with red roses and candlelight from my latest MegaScenes photo"
- "Put me on a rooftop at sunset in a red dress"
- "Create a black and white photo of the two of us in Paris at night"

## Data and privacy
What you give for the Valentine photo (the pictures you pick from your library and the details you give) goes to MegaScenes, stays in your MegaScenes account with the finished file, and is passed to the model provider that makes it. The plugin has no hooks or local scripts and sends nothing else.
