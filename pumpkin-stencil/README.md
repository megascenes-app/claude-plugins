# Pumpkin Carving Stencils

Create pumpkin carving stencils to print and trace onto your pumpkin: a spooky face, a black cat, a bat or a haunted house, in solid black shapes on white.

## What it does
Name what you want to carve and how detailed it should be: a grinning jack-o'-lantern face, a black cat on a moon, a haunted house with bats, an owl on a branch, with simple bold cuts, medium detail or intricate cuts for experienced carvers. You get a stencil with solid black areas to cut out on white, connected shapes with no floating pieces, centered in a pumpkin outline. For families, teachers and anyone carving pumpkins at home or for a Halloween party. Claude tells you the price first and makes it in your MegaScenes account when you say yes.

**How the files are made:** each pumpkin carving stencil is generated by an image model. Nothing is made until you say yes, and the price in credits comes first.

Limitations: a stencil as an image file, without text; it does not turn a photo into a stencil. Designs are spooky and fun, never gory, and original, not characters or brands owned by others. Making a file needs a signed-in MegaScenes account and runs in it; nothing is made until you confirm. Not for sexual or adult content or for real people's likeness. Files are made by generative models and saved in your MegaScenes library.

## Install
In Claude Code:
```
/plugin marketplace add megascenes-app/claude-plugins
/plugin install pumpkin-stencil@megascenes
```

## Connecting
- Pumpkin Carving Stencils talks to the MegaScenes server at `https://megascenes.com/api/claude/pumpkin-stencil/mcp` (Streamable HTTP).
- Connecting opens MegaScenes: sign in, create an account, or continue without one and add your email when you pay (OAuth with PKCE). Then you come straight back to the chat.
- Each pumpkin carving stencil uses credits from your MegaScenes account and is priced before it runs; new accounts can start with a low-cost trial week.
- The pumpkin carving stencils show in a viewer in the chat where Claude supports interactive apps; in Claude Code you get the links.

## Tools
| Tool | What it does |
|---|---|
| `list_pumpkin_stencil_models` | Models for pumpkin carving stencils with settings and starting prices. Read-only. |
| `estimate_pumpkin_stencil_cost` | Exact price of one pumpkin carving stencil before it runs. Read-only. |
| `create_pumpkin_stencil` | Starts one pumpkin carving stencil in your account. Uses credits; needs sign-in. |
| `get_pumpkin_stencil` | Status and file links. Read-only. |
| `get_account` | Your plan and credit balance. Read-only. |

## Data and privacy
What you give for the pumpkin carving stencil (the details you give) goes to MegaScenes, stays in your MegaScenes account with the finished file, and is passed to the model provider that makes it. The plugin has no hooks or local scripts and sends nothing else.
