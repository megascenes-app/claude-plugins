# Graduation Song Maker

Create an original graduation song with vocals for the graduate: their name, their school or degree and the memories of these years.

## What it does
Give the name to sing, the school or degree, a few memories of these years and the style: uplifting pop, acoustic, a rock anthem or hip hop, with a female or male lead, a duet or a choir. You get an original graduation song with vocals as an audio file, with lyrics written from your details or the lyrics you give. The voice sings the name you give. Give only the name and the details you want sung: no address or contact details are needed. For the graduation party, a slideshow, a video or a gift for the graduate. Claude tells you the price first and makes it in your MegaScenes account when you say yes.

**How the files are made:** each graduation song is generated by an audio model. Nothing is made until you say yes, and the price in credits comes first.

Limitations: original songs; it does not imitate a real singer's voice or copy an existing song or artist. Making a file needs a signed-in MegaScenes account and runs in it; nothing is made until you confirm. Not for sexual or adult content or for real people's likeness. Files are made by generative models and saved in your MegaScenes library.

## Install
In Claude Code:
```
/plugin marketplace add megascenes-app/claude-plugins
/plugin install graduation-song@megascenes
```

## Connecting
- Graduation Song Maker talks to the MegaScenes server at `https://megascenes.com/api/claude/graduation-song/mcp` (Streamable HTTP).
- Connecting opens MegaScenes: sign in, create an account, or continue without one and add your email when you pay (OAuth with PKCE). Then you come straight back to the chat.
- Each graduation song uses credits from your MegaScenes account and is priced before it runs; new accounts can start with a low-cost trial week.
- The graduation song plays in a viewer in the chat where Claude supports interactive apps; in Claude Code you get the link.

## Tools
| Tool | What it does |
|---|---|
| `list_graduation_song_models` | Models for graduation songs with settings and starting prices. Read-only. |
| `estimate_graduation_song_cost` | Exact price of one graduation song before it runs. Read-only. |
| `create_graduation_song` | Starts one graduation song in your account. Uses credits; needs sign-in. |
| `get_graduation_song` | Status and file links. Read-only. |
| `get_account` | Your plan and credit balance. Read-only. |

## Example prompts
- "An uplifting pop song for Sofia about late nights in the library and the friends she made"
- "A rock anthem for Leo and the class of 2027 about the first day and the last"
- "An acoustic song for Elena, who finished nursing school, sung as a duet"

## Data and privacy
What you give for the graduation song (the details you give) goes to MegaScenes, stays in your MegaScenes account with the finished file, and is passed to the model provider that makes it. The plugin has no hooks or local scripts and sends nothing else.
