# Birthday Photoshoot

Create birthday photos of yourself from your own photo in your MegaScenes library: the same face with balloons, a cake and candles or a glam party backdrop.

## What it does
Pick a selfie or a portrait of yourself from your MegaScenes library and choose the birthday scene: gold number balloons and confetti, blowing out the candles on a cake, a pink glam backdrop, a candid party shot, an editorial studio portrait. You get a picture that keeps your face, in the scene, outfit and style you chose. For your birthday post, profile pictures and party invitations. Claude tells you the price first and makes it in your MegaScenes account when you say yes.

**How the files are made:** each birthday photo is generated by an image model. Nothing is made until you say yes, and the price in credits comes first.

Limitations: only from your own photo or one of someone who agreed to it, never of a famous person; no swimwear, underwear or sensual shots. The photo must already be in your MegaScenes library; attachments from the chat are not passed to the plugin. Making a file needs a signed-in MegaScenes account and runs in it; nothing is made until you confirm. Not for sexual or adult content. Files are made by generative models and saved in your MegaScenes library.

## Install
In Claude Code:
```
/plugin marketplace add megascenes-app/claude-plugins
/plugin install birthday-photoshoot@megascenes
```

## Connecting
- Birthday Photoshoot talks to the MegaScenes server at `https://megascenes.com/api/claude/birthday-photoshoot/mcp` (Streamable HTTP).
- Connecting opens MegaScenes: sign in, create an account, or continue without one and add your email when you pay (OAuth with PKCE). Then you come straight back to the chat.
- Each birthday photo uses credits from your MegaScenes account and is priced before it runs; new accounts can start with a low-cost trial week.
- The birthday photos show in a viewer in the chat where Claude supports interactive apps; in Claude Code you get the links.

## Tools
| Tool | What it does |
|---|---|
| `list_birthday_photoshoot_models` | Models for birthday photos with settings and starting prices. Read-only. |
| `estimate_birthday_photoshoot_cost` | Exact price of one birthday photo before it runs. Read-only. |
| `create_birthday_photoshoot` | Starts one birthday photo in your account. Uses credits; needs sign-in. |
| `get_birthday_photoshoot` | Status and file links. Read-only. |
| `list_my_images` | Your uploads and images made here, to pick the one to start from. Read-only. |
| `get_account` | Your plan and credit balance. Read-only. |

## Example prompts
- "Make a birthday photo of me with gold balloons and confetti from my latest MegaScenes selfie"
- "Put me in front of a pink glam birthday backdrop in a sparkly dress"
- "Create a candid photo of me blowing out the candles on a cake"

## Data and privacy
What you give for the birthday photo (the picture you pick from your library and the details you give) goes to MegaScenes, stays in your MegaScenes account with the finished file, and is passed to the model provider that makes it. The plugin has no hooks or local scripts and sends nothing else.
