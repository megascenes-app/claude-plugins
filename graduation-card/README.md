# Graduation Card Maker

Design graduation cards for the graduate in your life, with their name, your congratulations and your signature in the design.

## What it does
Give who the card is for, the main line, a short message and how you sign it, and pick the look: black and gold, confetti and caps, elegant navy, bright and fun. You get a graduation card with your words set in the design, portrait, square or wide, ready to print, post or send. Give only the words you want printed on the card: no addresses or contact details are needed. For parents, grandparents, friends and teachers congratulating a graduate. Claude tells you the price first and makes it in your MegaScenes account when you say yes.

**How the files are made:** each graduation card is generated by an image model. Nothing is made until you say yes, and the price in credits comes first.

Limitations: designs as image files; your text is passed to the model as written, so check the lettering in the result. It does not mail the cards. Making a file needs a signed-in MegaScenes account and runs in it; nothing is made until you confirm. Not for sexual or adult content or for real people's likeness. Files are made by generative models and saved in your MegaScenes library.

## Install
In Claude Code:
```
/plugin marketplace add megascenes-app/claude-plugins
/plugin install graduation-card@megascenes
```

## Connecting
- Graduation Card Maker talks to the MegaScenes server at `https://megascenes.com/api/claude/graduation-card/mcp` (Streamable HTTP).
- Connecting opens MegaScenes: sign in, create an account, or continue without one and add your email when you pay (OAuth with PKCE). Then you come straight back to the chat.
- Each graduation card uses credits from your MegaScenes account and is priced before it runs; new accounts can start with a low-cost trial week.
- The graduation cards show in a viewer in the chat where Claude supports interactive apps; in Claude Code you get the links.

## Tools
| Tool | What it does |
|---|---|
| `list_graduation_card_models` | Models for graduation cards with settings and starting prices. Read-only. |
| `estimate_graduation_card_cost` | Exact price of one graduation card before it runs. Read-only. |
| `create_graduation_card` | Starts one graduation card in your account. Uses credits; needs sign-in. |
| `get_graduation_card` | Status and file links. Read-only. |
| `get_account` | Your plan and credit balance. Read-only. |

## Example prompts
- "A black and gold graduation card for Sofia that says Congratulations Graduate, signed Love, Mom and Dad"
- "A bright and fun graduation card for Leo: You did it! Class of 2027"
- "An elegant navy graduation card with the words The best is yet to come"

## Data and privacy
What you give for the graduation card (the details you give) goes to MegaScenes, stays in your MegaScenes account with the finished file, and is passed to the model provider that makes it. The plugin has no hooks or local scripts and sends nothing else.
