# Anniversary Party Invitations

Design invitations to a wedding anniversary party with the couple's names, the years together, the date and the place on them, from a silver 25th to a golden 50th.

## What it does
Give the couple's names, the years they are celebrating and the party details you want printed, and pick the look: golden and elegant, silver and white, romantic florals, vintage, minimal. You get an invitation with your words set in the design and the years as a large number, portrait to print, tall to send from your phone, or square. Give only the words you want printed on the invitation: no guest list or contact details are needed. For couples and for the families who throw the party for them, from a 10th to a 60th anniversary. Claude tells you the price first and makes it in your MegaScenes account when you say yes.

**How the files are made:** each anniversary party invitation is generated by an image model. Nothing is made until you say yes, and the price in credits comes first.

Limitations: designs as image files; your text is passed to the model as written, so check the lettering in the result. It does not send the invitations, keep guest lists or track replies. Making a file needs a signed-in MegaScenes account and runs in it; nothing is made until you confirm. Not for sexual or adult content or for real people's likeness. Files are made by generative models and saved in your MegaScenes library.

## Install
In Claude Code:
```
/plugin marketplace add megascenes-app/claude-plugins
/plugin install anniversary-invitation@megascenes
```

## Connecting
- Anniversary Party Invitations talks to the MegaScenes server at `https://megascenes.com/api/claude/anniversary-invitation/mcp` (Streamable HTTP).
- Connecting opens MegaScenes: sign in, create an account, or continue without one and add your email when you pay (OAuth with PKCE). Then you come straight back to the chat.
- Each anniversary party invitation uses credits from your MegaScenes account and is priced before it runs; new accounts can start with a low-cost trial week.
- The anniversary party invitations show in a viewer in the chat where Claude supports interactive apps; in Claude Code you get the links.

## Tools
| Tool | What it does |
|---|---|
| `list_anniversary_invitation_models` | Models for anniversary party invitations with settings and starting prices. Read-only. |
| `estimate_anniversary_invitation_cost` | Exact price of one anniversary party invitation before it runs. Read-only. |
| `create_anniversary_invitation` | Starts one anniversary party invitation in your account. Uses credits; needs sign-in. |
| `get_anniversary_invitation` | Status and file links. Read-only. |
| `get_account` | Your plan and credit balance. Read-only. |

## Example prompts

- "A silver and white invitation for the 25th anniversary of Anna and Luca"
- "A romantic floral invitation for the 10th anniversary party of Elena and Marco"

## Data and privacy
What you give for the anniversary party invitation (the details you give) goes to MegaScenes, stays in your MegaScenes account with the finished file, and is passed to the model provider that makes it. The plugin has no hooks or local scripts and sends nothing else.
