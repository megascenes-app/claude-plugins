# MegaScenes plugins for Claude

Images, videos, logos, voice-overs, music and design assets for Claude, made in your MegaScenes account with the price shown before every run.

| Plugin | What it does |
|---|---|
| [MegaScenes](./megascenes) | Images, videos, voice-overs and music from 140+ models (Veo, Kling, Seedance, Nano Banana, ElevenLabs) in one place, with the exact price before every run. Generated in your MegaScenes account. |
| [Image](./image) | Create images from a description: photos, pictures, illustrations, art, characters and scenes in any aspect ratio. Generated in seconds and saved to your MegaScenes library. |
| [Video](./video) | Create short video clips from a description of the scene and camera: reels, TikToks, shorts, ads, intros and stories. Generated with Kling, Veo, Seedance, Wan and more. |
| [Logo Maker](./logo-maker) | Logo maker: design logo concepts from your brand name, business, style and colors. Up to four options per run, generated as image files you can download. |
| [Image to Video](./image-to-video) | Image to video: animate a photo or picture from your MegaScenes library into a short clip with the motion and camera move you describe, generated in a few minutes. |
| [Video Editor](./video-editor) | Video editor: edit a video from your MegaScenes library with a prompt. Restyle it, swap objects or the background, reframe it to 9:16 or 1:1, extend it with new footage, or lip-sync it to a voice track. |
| [Text to Speech](./text-to-speech) | Text to speech: turn a script into natural voice-overs and narration in many voices and languages, generated as audio files for videos, ads, podcasts and audiobooks. |
| [Music](./music) | Create original background music and soundtracks from a description of genre, mood, instruments and tempo, generated as audio files for videos, podcasts, games and ads. |
| [Sound Effects](./sound-effects) | Sound effects generator: describe a whoosh, footsteps, rain ambience, foley or a UI click and get the SFX file generated in seconds, for videos, games, apps and podcasts. |
| [Image Upscaler](./image-upscaler) | Image upscaler: enlarge, enhance and sharpen an image from your MegaScenes library to 2x or 4x for print, wallpapers and 4K screens. The larger file is generated in seconds. |
| [Poster](./poster) | Poster and flyer maker: event posters, flyers, sale announcements and menus generated with your headline and details spelled out correctly. |
| [Sticker Maker](./sticker-maker) | Sticker maker: die-cut stickers of characters, pets, quotes and logos with a white border, kawaii or retro, generated ready to print or share. |
| [Emoji Maker](./emoji-maker) | Emoji maker: custom emoji generated from your description in the glossy emoji look, for Slack, Discord and chats. |
| [Pixel Art Maker](./pixel-art-maker) | Pixel art maker: 8-bit, 16-bit and isometric pixel art, game sprites, items and retro scenes generated from your description. |
| [Tattoo Generator](./tattoo-generator) | Tattoo generator: your idea turned into clean tattoo flash art in fine line, traditional, blackwork, Japanese or geometric style, generated as designs to take to your artist. |
| [Character Creator](./character-creator) | Character creator: original characters for stories, games, comics and OCs, generated as portraits, full body art or turnaround sheets. |
| [Avatar Maker](./avatar-maker) | Avatar maker: profile picture avatars (PFP) generated from a description in 3D cartoon, anime, flat, comic or pixel styles. |
| [Meme](./meme) | Meme generator: original meme images about any situation, generated with your top and bottom captions spelled out. |
| [Thumbnail Maker](./thumbnail-maker) | Thumbnail maker: eye-catching 16:9 YouTube thumbnails generated from your video title, with bold text on the image. |
| [Coloring Page Maker](./coloring-page-maker) | Coloring page maker: printable coloring pages of any subject, simple for kids or detailed mandalas for adults, generated ready to print. |
| [Song](./song) | Song maker: an original song with vocals, generated from a theme or your own lyrics in the genre and mood you choose. |
| [Beat](./beat) | Beat maker: instrumental hip hop, trap, lo-fi, drill, afrobeats and house beats generated at the tempo and mood you choose. |
| [Book Cover Maker](./book-cover-maker) | Book cover maker: covers for novels, ebooks and self-published KDP books, generated with your title and author name spelled out. |
| [Album Cover Maker](./album-cover-maker) | Album cover maker: square cover art for albums, EPs and singles, generated with the artist name and title, ready for Spotify and other streaming platforms. |
| [Invitation Maker](./invitation-maker) | Invitation maker: wedding, birthday, baby shower and party invitations and greeting cards, generated with your details spelled out. |
| [Photo Editor](./photo-editor) | Photo editor: edit a photo or image from your MegaScenes library by describing the change. Swap the background, remove or add objects, change colors or style, retouch, or combine images. |
| [Background Remover](./background-remover) | Background remover: cut the subject out of a photo or image from your MegaScenes library and get it on a transparent PNG, for product shots, stickers, profile pictures and designs. |
| [Business Card Maker](./business-card-maker) | Business card maker: design the front of a business card with your name, job title and contact details spelled out, in the look you want, ready to print or share. |
| [Interior Design](./interior-design) | Interior design: redesign a room in the style you want, from a photo of the room in your MegaScenes library or from a description. Living rooms, bedrooms, kitchens, bathrooms and offices. |
| [Floor Plans](./floor-plans) | Floor plans: draw an illustrative floor plan of a home, apartment or office from the rooms you list, or redraw a sketch from your MegaScenes library. A visual guide, not a construction drawing. |
| [Trading Cards](./trading-cards) | Trading cards: design a custom trading card with your card name, stats and artwork, from a description or a photo in your MegaScenes library. Creatures, heroes, pets and game cards. |
| [Voice Changer](./voice-changer) | Voice changer: change the voice of a recording from your MegaScenes library to one of the ready-made voices, keeping its words, timing and emotion. It never copies a real person's voice. |
| [Vocal Remover](./vocal-remover) | Vocal remover: split a song from your MegaScenes library into the instrumental and the vocals, two files for karaoke, covers, practice and remixes, or into six stems. |
| [Collage Maker](./collage-maker) | Collage maker: arrange 2 to 14 photos from your MegaScenes library into one collage in the layout and style you describe, for birthdays, trips, mood boards and social posts. |
| [Image Enhancer](./image-enhancer) | Image enhancer: sharpen and restore a blurry, noisy or old photo from your MegaScenes library, with cleaner detail, less grain and restored faces, at a higher resolution. |
| [Graffiti Generator](./graffiti-generator) | Graffiti generator: turn a word or a name into graffiti art in wildstyle, bubble letters, 3D tags or throw-ups, on a wall, a train or a clean background. |
| [Professional Headshots](./professional-headshots) | Professional headshots: a studio headshot of yourself from your own photo in your MegaScenes library, with the same face and the outfit and background you pick, for profiles and resumes. |
| [Landscape Design](./landscape-design) | Landscape design: redesign a garden, backyard, patio, terrace or front yard in the style you want, from a photo in your MegaScenes library or from a description. |
| [Family Crests](./family-crests) | Family crests: design a family crest or coat of arms with your surname and motto, and the symbols and colors you choose, for gifts, reunions, prints and engravings. |
| [Line Art](./line-art) | Line art: turn a photo from your MegaScenes library or a description into clean black outlines with no shading, for illustrations, tattoo stencils, embroidery, laser cutting and prints. |
| [Image Translator](./image-translator) | Image translator: translate the text inside an image from your MegaScenes library and get the same image back with the translation in place, for comics, menus, signs, posters and slides. |
| [Audio Translator](./audio-translator) | Audio translator: translate a recording from your MegaScenes library into another language, spoken in the voices of the original speakers. Podcasts, voice-overs, lessons, up to 8 minutes. |
| [Channel Banners](./channel-banners) | Channel banners: design a YouTube channel banner with your channel name and tagline at 2560 x 1440, the text kept in the safe area that shows on phones, desktops and TVs. |
| [Flyer Maker](./flyer-maker) | Flyer maker: design a flyer for your event, sale or business with the headline, date, place and offer in plain view, on a portrait page ready to print or post. |
| [Fantasy Maps](./fantasy-maps) | Fantasy maps: an illustrated map of a world you invent, for tabletop games, novels and campaigns, with kingdoms, coasts, mountains, forests, rivers and your place names. |
| [Photo Filters](./photo-filters) | Photo filters: turn a photo from your MegaScenes library into a new look, such as cartoon, anime, watercolor, oil painting, pencil sketch, pop art or vintage film, keeping its composition. |
| [3D Animation](./3d-animation) | 3D animation: a short clip in the look of a 3D animated film, from a description or a picture in your MegaScenes library, with stylized characters, soft lighting and a cinematic camera. |
| [Family Portraits](./family-portraits) | Family portraits: one portrait of your family from photos in your MegaScenes library, everyone with their own face, in the style and setting you choose, from studio to painted. |
| [Pet Portraits](./pet-portraits) | Pet portraits: turn a photo of your pet from your MegaScenes library into a regal, Renaissance, watercolor or cartoon portrait, keeping its breed, colors and markings. |
| [Photoshoot Generator](./photoshoot-generator) | Photoshoot generator: new photos of yourself from your own photo in your MegaScenes library, with the same face in the scene and outfit you choose, for profiles, portfolios and posts. |
| [Hairstyle Try On](./hairstyle-try-on) | Hairstyle try on: see a new haircut or hair color on your own photo from your MegaScenes library before the salon, with the same face: a bob, bangs, a buzz cut, waves or a new shade. |
| [Monogram Maker](./monogram-maker) | Monogram maker: design a monogram from your initials for weddings, stationery, embroidery, signet rings and personal brands, in classic, modern or floral styles. |
| [Social Media Posts](./social-media-posts) | Social media posts: design a post with your message on it, a square feed post, a tall story or a pin, for an announcement, a quote, a sale or a tip. |
| [Banner Maker](./banner-maker) | Banner maker: design a banner with your headline for a website, a store, an event or a sale, in a wide, square or tall format. |
| [Ringtone Maker](./ringtone-maker) | Ringtone maker: an original ringtone or notification tune of about 30 seconds from a style and mood, a catchy loop, marimba, chiptune, lo-fi or orchestral. |

Install in Claude Code:

```
/plugin marketplace add megascenes-app/claude-plugins
/plugin install <plugin>@megascenes
```

Each plugin connects Claude to the MegaScenes server for that use case. Prices are shown in credits before anything is made, and new accounts can start with a low-cost trial week.
