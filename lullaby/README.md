# Lullaby Maker

Create an original lullaby with a soft voice that sings your little one's name, slow and soothing, with music box, piano, guitar or harp, to play at bedtime.

## What it does
Give the name to sing softly, a few things to sing about, such as the moon and the stars, the sea or the family cat, and the sound you like: a music box, soft piano, acoustic guitar or harp, with a female or male voice. You get an original lullaby as an audio file, slow and calm, with lyrics written from your details or the lyrics you give. The voice sings the name you give. Give only the name and the details you want sung: no address or other personal details are needed. For parents and grandparents, for bedtime, naps and a gift for a new baby. Claude tells you the price first and makes it in your MegaScenes account when you say yes.

**How the files are made:** each lullaby is generated by an audio model. Nothing is made until you say yes, and the price in credits comes first.

Limitations: original lullabies; it does not imitate a real singer's voice or copy an existing lullaby, song or artist. Making a file needs a signed-in MegaScenes account and runs in it; nothing is made until you confirm. Not for sexual or adult content or for real people's likeness. Files are made by generative models and saved in your MegaScenes library.

## Install
In Claude Code:
```
/plugin marketplace add megascenes-app/claude-plugins
/plugin install lullaby@megascenes
```

## Connecting
- Lullaby Maker talks to the MegaScenes server at `https://megascenes.com/api/claude/lullaby/mcp` (Streamable HTTP).
- Connecting opens MegaScenes: sign in, create an account, or continue without one and add your email when you pay (OAuth with PKCE). Then you come straight back to the chat.
- Each lullaby uses credits from your MegaScenes account and is priced before it runs; new accounts can start with a low-cost trial week.
- The lullaby plays in a viewer in the chat where Claude supports interactive apps; in Claude Code you get the link.

## Tools
| Tool | What it does |
|---|---|
| `list_lullaby_models` | Models for lullabies with settings and starting prices. Read-only. |
| `estimate_lullaby_cost` | Exact price of one lullaby before it runs. Read-only. |
| `create_lullaby` | Starts one lullaby in your account. Uses credits; needs sign-in. |
| `get_lullaby` | Status and file links. Read-only. |
| `get_account` | Your plan and credit balance. Read-only. |

## Example prompts
- "A lullaby for Leo with a music box, about the moon and the stars"
- "A soft piano lullaby that sings the name Sofia, with a male voice"
- "A gentle guitar lullaby about the sea and our cat Pepper"

## Data and privacy
What you give for the lullaby (the details you give) goes to MegaScenes, stays in your MegaScenes account with the finished file, and is passed to the model provider that makes it. The plugin has no hooks or local scripts and sends nothing else.
