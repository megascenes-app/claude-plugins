# Valentine Song Maker

Create an original Valentine's Day song with vocals for the person you love: their name, your story and what you love about them, to send on February 14th.

## What it does
Give the name to sing, a few lines about your story and what you love about them, and the style: R&B, acoustic, a pop ballad, soul, jazz or a funny pop song, with a female or male lead or a duet. You get an original Valentine's Day song with vocals as an audio file, with lyrics written from your story or the lyrics you give. The voice sings the name you give. Give only the name and the details you want sung: no address or contact details are needed. To send on February 14th, play at a dinner for two or add to a Valentine's video. Claude tells you the price first and makes it in your MegaScenes account when you say yes.

**How the files are made:** each Valentine song is generated by an audio model. Nothing is made until you say yes, and the price in credits comes first.

Limitations: original songs; it does not imitate a real singer's voice or copy an existing song or artist. Making a file needs a signed-in MegaScenes account and runs in it; nothing is made until you confirm. Not for sexual or adult content or for real people's likeness. Files are made by generative models and saved in your MegaScenes library.

## Install
In Claude Code:
```
/plugin marketplace add megascenes-app/claude-plugins
/plugin install valentine-song@megascenes
```

## Connecting
- Valentine Song Maker talks to the MegaScenes server at `https://megascenes.com/api/claude/valentine-song/mcp` (Streamable HTTP).
- Connecting opens MegaScenes: sign in, create an account, or continue without one and add your email when you pay (OAuth with PKCE). Then you come straight back to the chat.
- Each Valentine song uses credits from your MegaScenes account and is priced before it runs; new accounts can start with a low-cost trial week.
- The Valentine song plays in a viewer in the chat where Claude supports interactive apps; in Claude Code you get the link.

## Tools
| Tool | What it does |
|---|---|
| `list_valentine_song_models` | Models for Valentine songs with settings and starting prices. Read-only. |
| `estimate_valentine_song_cost` | Exact price of one Valentine song before it runs. Read-only. |
| `create_valentine_song` | Starts one Valentine song in your account. Uses credits; needs sign-in. |
| `get_valentine_song` | Status and file links. Read-only. |
| `get_account` | Your plan and credit balance. Read-only. |

## Example prompts
- "A Valentine's R&B song for Sofia about our first date in the rain"
- "A funny pop Valentine song for Luca about his terrible jokes"
- "An acoustic duet for Elena for our first Valentine's Day together"

## Data and privacy
What you give for the Valentine song (the details you give) goes to MegaScenes, stays in your MegaScenes account with the finished file, and is passed to the model provider that makes it. The plugin has no hooks or local scripts and sends nothing else.
