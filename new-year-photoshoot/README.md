# New Year Photoshoot

Create New Year's Eve photos of yourself from your own selfie in your MegaScenes library: the same face with fireworks behind you, gold confetti, sparklers or a glam party backdrop.

## What it does
Pick a selfie or a portrait of yourself from your MegaScenes library and choose the scene: fireworks over the city at midnight, gold confetti falling, holding sparklers, a glam black and gold backdrop, with gold number balloons for the new year if you like, in a sequin dress, a velvet blazer or a party hat, as a glam studio shot, a candid party photo, an editorial portrait or vintage film. You get a picture that keeps your face, in the scene, outfit and style you chose, portrait, square or tall for stories. For your New Year post, profile pictures and party invitations. Claude tells you the price first and makes it in your MegaScenes account when you say yes.

**How the files are made:** each New Year photo is generated by an image model. Nothing is made until you say yes, and the price in credits comes first.

Limitations: only from a selfie or portrait of yourself, never of a famous person; no swimwear, underwear or sensual shots. The year on the balloons is passed to the model as written, so check the lettering in the result. The photo must already be in your MegaScenes library; attachments from the chat are not passed to the plugin. Making a file needs a signed-in MegaScenes account and runs in it; nothing is made until you confirm. Not for sexual or adult content. Files are made by generative models and saved in your MegaScenes library.

## Install
In Claude Code:
```
/plugin marketplace add megascenes-app/claude-plugins
/plugin install new-year-photoshoot@megascenes
```

## Connecting
- New Year Photoshoot talks to the MegaScenes server at `https://megascenes.com/api/claude/new-year-photoshoot/mcp` (Streamable HTTP).
- Connecting opens MegaScenes: sign in, create an account, or continue without one and add your email when you pay (OAuth with PKCE). Then you come straight back to the chat.
- Each New Year photo uses credits from your MegaScenes account and is priced before it runs; new accounts can start with a low-cost trial week.
- The New Year photos show in a viewer in the chat where Claude supports interactive apps; in Claude Code you get the links.

## Tools
| Tool | What it does |
|---|---|
| `list_new_year_photoshoot_models` | Models for New Year photos with settings and starting prices. Read-only. |
| `estimate_new_year_photoshoot_cost` | Exact price of one New Year photo before it runs. Read-only. |
| `create_new_year_photoshoot` | Starts one New Year photo in your account. Uses credits; needs sign-in. |
| `get_new_year_photoshoot` | Status and file links. Read-only. |
| `list_my_images` | Your uploads and images made here, to pick the one to start from. Read-only. |
| `get_account` | Your plan and credit balance. Read-only. |

## Data and privacy
What you give for the New Year photo (the picture you pick from your library and the details you give) goes to MegaScenes, stays in your MegaScenes account with the finished file, and is passed to the model provider that makes it. The plugin has no hooks or local scripts and sends nothing else.
