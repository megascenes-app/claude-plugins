# Valentine Wallpapers

Create Valentine's Day wallpapers for your phone or computer: hearts, roses, soft pink skies and candlelight, in the style you choose.

## What it does
Describe the Valentine's scene you want behind your apps: floating pink hearts over a sunset sky, red roses on dark velvet, cozy candlelight, a minimal pattern of little hearts, as a photo, watercolor, minimal flat, cute cartoon or dreamy pastel. You get a wallpaper in the shape you need, tall for a phone or wide for a computer, with a calm area at the top for the clock. For phones, laptops, lock screens and matching wallpapers for the two of you. Claude tells you the price first and makes it in your MegaScenes account when you say yes.

**How the files are made:** each Valentine wallpaper is generated by an image model. Nothing is made until you say yes, and the price in credits comes first.

Limitations: one image per run, without text; it does not set the wallpaper on your device for you. Making a file needs a signed-in MegaScenes account and runs in it; nothing is made until you confirm. Not for sexual or adult content or for real people's likeness. Files are made by generative models and saved in your MegaScenes library.

## Install
In Claude Code:
```
/plugin marketplace add megascenes-app/claude-plugins
/plugin install valentine-wallpaper@megascenes
```

## Connecting
- Valentine Wallpapers talks to the MegaScenes server at `https://megascenes.com/api/claude/valentine-wallpaper/mcp` (Streamable HTTP).
- Connecting opens MegaScenes: sign in, create an account, or continue without one and add your email when you pay (OAuth with PKCE). Then you come straight back to the chat.
- Each Valentine wallpaper uses credits from your MegaScenes account and is priced before it runs; new accounts can start with a low-cost trial week.
- The Valentine wallpapers show in a viewer in the chat where Claude supports interactive apps; in Claude Code you get the links.

## Tools
| Tool | What it does |
|---|---|
| `list_valentine_wallpaper_models` | Models for Valentine wallpapers with settings and starting prices. Read-only. |
| `estimate_valentine_wallpaper_cost` | Exact price of one Valentine wallpaper before it runs. Read-only. |
| `create_valentine_wallpaper` | Starts one Valentine wallpaper in your account. Uses credits; needs sign-in. |
| `get_valentine_wallpaper` | Status and file links. Read-only. |
| `get_account` | Your plan and credit balance. Read-only. |

## Example prompts
- "A Valentine phone wallpaper of floating pink hearts over a sunset sky"
- "Red roses on dark velvet as a wide desktop background"
- "A minimal pattern of little red hearts on blush pink for my phone"

## Data and privacy
What you give for the Valentine wallpaper (the details you give) goes to MegaScenes, stays in your MegaScenes account with the finished file, and is passed to the model provider that makes it. The plugin has no hooks or local scripts and sends nothing else.
