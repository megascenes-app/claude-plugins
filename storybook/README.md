# Storybook Maker

Illustrate a short picture book page by page, with the same characters and the same style on every page, from a story you write in the chat.

## What it does
Write a short story in the chat with Claude, a few pages with characters of your own, then illustrate it one page at a time: say what happens on the page, who the characters are and the style, soft watercolor, bold flat colors or colored pencil. From the second page on, the first page is passed as a reference so the characters and the style stay the same. You get one illustrated page per run, landscape, portrait or square. The text of the story stays in the chat, not on the pictures, so you can set it under each page in the layout you like. For parents, grandparents and teachers making a story of their own, for bedtime, a gift or a class project. Claude tells you the price first and makes it in your MegaScenes account when you say yes.

**How the files are made:** each storybook page is generated by an image model. Nothing is made until you say yes, and the price in credits comes first.

Limitations: one illustrated page per run, without the story text on it; original characters only, not characters or brands owned by others. It does not print or bind the book. Making a file needs a signed-in MegaScenes account and runs in it; nothing is made until you confirm. Not for sexual or adult content or for real people's likeness. Files are made by generative models and saved in your MegaScenes library.

## Install
In Claude Code:
```
/plugin marketplace add megascenes-app/claude-plugins
/plugin install storybook@megascenes
```

## Connecting
- Storybook Maker talks to the MegaScenes server at `https://megascenes.com/api/claude/storybook/mcp` (Streamable HTTP).
- Connecting opens MegaScenes: sign in, create an account, or continue without one and add your email when you pay (OAuth with PKCE). Then you come straight back to the chat.
- Each storybook page uses credits from your MegaScenes account and is priced before it runs; new accounts can start with a low-cost trial week.
- The storybook pages show in a viewer in the chat where Claude supports interactive apps; in Claude Code you get the links.

## Tools
| Tool | What it does |
|---|---|
| `list_storybook_page_models` | Models for storybook pages with settings and starting prices. Read-only. |
| `estimate_storybook_page_cost` | Exact price of one storybook page before it runs. Read-only. |
| `create_storybook_page` | Starts one storybook page in your account. Uses credits; needs sign-in. |
| `get_storybook_page` | Status and file links. Read-only. |
| `list_my_images` | Your uploads and images made here, to pick the ones to start from. Read-only. |
| `get_account` | Your plan and credit balance. Read-only. |

## Example prompts
- "Help me write a short story about Mia and her fox friend Pip, then illustrate the first page"
- "Illustrate page 2: Mia and Pip find a glowing door in the forest"
- "A soft watercolor picture book page of a little bear who is afraid of the dark"

## Data and privacy
What you give for the storybook page (the pictures you pick from your library, if any, and the details you give) goes to MegaScenes, stays in your MegaScenes account with the finished file, and is passed to the model provider that makes it. The plugin has no hooks or local scripts and sends nothing else.
