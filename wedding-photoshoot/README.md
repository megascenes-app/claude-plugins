# Wedding Photoshoot

Create pre-wedding and wedding photos of the two of you from your own photos in your MegaScenes library: the same faces on a beach at sunset, in a vineyard or in a city at night.

## What it does
Pick one photo of the two of you, or one photo of each, from your MegaScenes library and choose the setting, the outfits and the style: a vineyard at golden hour, a beach at sunset, a Paris street at night, a garden with an arch of flowers, in a wedding dress and suit or casual linen, romantic editorial, candid film or black and white. You get a picture that keeps both faces, in the scene you chose. For save the dates, wedding websites, invitations and albums. Claude tells you the price first and makes it in your MegaScenes account when you say yes.

**How the files are made:** each wedding photo is generated by an image model. Nothing is made until you say yes, and the price in credits comes first.

Limitations: only the couple from your own photos, with the consent of both, never a famous person; no swimwear, underwear or sensual shots. One or two photos, already in your MegaScenes library; attachments from the chat are not passed to the plugin. Making a file needs a signed-in MegaScenes account and runs in it; nothing is made until you confirm. Not for sexual or adult content. Files are made by generative models and saved in your MegaScenes library.

## Install
In Claude Code:
```
/plugin marketplace add megascenes-app/claude-plugins
/plugin install wedding-photoshoot@megascenes
```

## Connecting
- Wedding Photoshoot talks to the MegaScenes server at `https://megascenes.com/api/claude/wedding-photoshoot/mcp` (Streamable HTTP).
- Connecting opens MegaScenes: sign in, create an account, or continue without one and add your email when you pay (OAuth with PKCE). Then you come straight back to the chat.
- Each wedding photo uses credits from your MegaScenes account and is priced before it runs; new accounts can start with a low-cost trial week.
- The wedding photos show in a viewer in the chat where Claude supports interactive apps; in Claude Code you get the links.

## Tools
| Tool | What it does |
|---|---|
| `list_wedding_photoshoot_models` | Models for wedding photos with settings and starting prices. Read-only. |
| `estimate_wedding_photoshoot_cost` | Exact price of one wedding photo before it runs. Read-only. |
| `create_wedding_photoshoot` | Starts one wedding photo in your account. Uses credits; needs sign-in. |
| `get_wedding_photoshoot` | Status and file links. Read-only. |
| `list_my_images` | Your uploads and images made here, to pick the ones to start from. Read-only. |
| `get_account` | Your plan and credit balance. Read-only. |

## Example prompts
- "Make a pre-wedding photo of us in a vineyard at golden hour from my latest MegaScenes photo"
- "Put the two of us on a beach at sunset in a white dress and a navy suit"
- "Create a black and white photo of us on a Paris street at night"

## Data and privacy
What you give for the wedding photo (the pictures you pick from your library and the details you give) goes to MegaScenes, stays in your MegaScenes account with the finished file, and is passed to the model provider that makes it. The plugin has no hooks or local scripts and sends nothing else.
