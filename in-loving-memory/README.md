# In Loving Memory

Design memorial cards, funeral program covers and remembrance signs in loving memory of someone, with their name, the dates and the words you choose.

## What it does
Give the name, the dates and the words you want, a verse, a quote or a short message, religious or not, and choose the item and the imagery: a memorial card, a funeral program cover, a remembrance sign or a thank-you card, with white lilies, a dove, a candle, a sunset over the sea or an olive tree. You get a calm, respectful design with your words set in it, portrait, square or wide. The words are yours to choose. Give only what you want on the design: no other personal details are needed. For families and friends preparing a funeral, a memorial service or a remembrance. Claude tells you the price first and makes it in your MegaScenes account when you say yes.

**How the files are made:** each memorial card is generated by an image model. Nothing is made until you say yes, and the price in credits comes first.

Limitations: text and gentle imagery only, with no photo or portrait of the person. Your text is passed to the model as written, so check the lettering in the result. It does not print, mail or publish the design. Making a file needs a signed-in MegaScenes account and runs in it; nothing is made until you confirm. Not for sexual or adult content or for real people's likeness. Files are made by generative models and saved in your MegaScenes library.

## Install
In Claude Code:
```
/plugin marketplace add megascenes-app/claude-plugins
/plugin install in-loving-memory@megascenes
```

## Connecting
- In Loving Memory talks to the MegaScenes server at `https://megascenes.com/api/claude/in-loving-memory/mcp` (Streamable HTTP).
- Connecting opens MegaScenes: sign in, create an account, or continue without one and add your email when you pay (OAuth with PKCE). Then you come straight back to the chat.
- Each memorial card uses credits from your MegaScenes account and is priced before it runs; new accounts can start with a low-cost trial week.
- The memorial cards show in a viewer in the chat where Claude supports interactive apps; in Claude Code you get the links.

## Tools
| Tool | What it does |
|---|---|
| `list_memorial_card_models` | Models for memorial cards with settings and starting prices. Read-only. |
| `estimate_memorial_card_cost` | Exact price of one memorial card before it runs. Read-only. |
| `create_memorial_card` | Starts one memorial card in your account. Uses credits; needs sign-in. |
| `get_memorial_card` | Status and file links. Read-only. |
| `get_account` | Your plan and credit balance. Read-only. |

## Example prompts
- "A memorial card in loving memory of Rosa Bianchi, 1941 - 2026, with white lilies"
- "A funeral program cover with a dove and the words Forever in our hearts"
- "A remembrance sign with a candle and a short verse I will write"

## Data and privacy
What you give for the memorial card (the details you give) goes to MegaScenes, stays in your MegaScenes account with the finished file, and is passed to the model provider that makes it. The plugin has no hooks or local scripts and sends nothing else.
