# New Year Greeting Images

Create Happy New Year images with your own wishes and the year written in the picture, ready to send in chats and share on social media.

## What it does
Write the wish you want to share, such as Happy New Year 2027 or a line of your own, and pick the scene and the look: fireworks over a city skyline, gold confetti, sparklers in the night, a snowy village at midnight, in elegant gold and black, neon, watercolor, photo or glitter. You get a festive image with your words in it, square, tall for stories and status, or wide. For group chats, status updates, stories and New Year posts. Claude tells you the price first and makes it in your MegaScenes account when you say yes.

**How the files are made:** each New Year greeting image is generated by an image model. Nothing is made until you say yes, and the price in credits comes first.

Limitations: one image per run; your text is passed to the model as written, so check the lettering in the result. It does not post or send the image for you. Making a file needs a signed-in MegaScenes account and runs in it; nothing is made until you confirm. Not for sexual or adult content or for real people's likeness. Files are made by generative models and saved in your MegaScenes library.

## Install
In Claude Code:
```
/plugin marketplace add megascenes-app/claude-plugins
/plugin install new-year-greeting@megascenes
```

## Connecting
- New Year Greeting Images talks to the MegaScenes server at `https://megascenes.com/api/claude/new-year-greeting/mcp` (Streamable HTTP).
- Connecting opens MegaScenes: sign in, create an account, or continue without one and add your email when you pay (OAuth with PKCE). Then you come straight back to the chat.
- Each New Year greeting image uses credits from your MegaScenes account and is priced before it runs; new accounts can start with a low-cost trial week.
- The New Year greeting images show in a viewer in the chat where Claude supports interactive apps; in Claude Code you get the links.

## Tools
| Tool | What it does |
|---|---|
| `list_new_year_greeting_models` | Models for New Year greeting images with settings and starting prices. Read-only. |
| `estimate_new_year_greeting_cost` | Exact price of one New Year greeting image before it runs. Read-only. |
| `create_new_year_greeting` | Starts one New Year greeting image in your account. Uses credits; needs sign-in. |
| `get_new_year_greeting` | Status and file links. Read-only. |
| `get_account` | Your plan and credit balance. Read-only. |

## Data and privacy
What you give for the New Year greeting image (the details you give) goes to MegaScenes, stays in your MegaScenes account with the finished file, and is passed to the model provider that makes it. The plugin has no hooks or local scripts and sends nothing else.
