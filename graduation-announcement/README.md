# Graduation Announcements

Design graduation announcements with the graduate's name, the degree or diploma, the year and the school, to post, print or send to family and friends.

## What it does
Give the graduate's name, the degree or diploma, the year or class and the school, and pick the look: elegant black and gold, classic navy, modern minimal, photo-style. You get an announcement with your words set in the design, with caps and laurels, portrait, square or tall for stories. Give only the words you want printed: no addresses or contact details are needed. For graduates and proud families sharing the news, from high school to a doctorate. Claude tells you the price first and makes it in your MegaScenes account when you say yes.

**How the files are made:** each graduation announcement is generated by an image model. Nothing is made until you say yes, and the price in credits comes first.

Limitations: designs as image files; your text is passed to the model as written, so check the lettering in the result. A school name is written only as you give it, and crests and logos are not drawn. It does not post or mail the announcement. Making a file needs a signed-in MegaScenes account and runs in it; nothing is made until you confirm. Not for sexual or adult content or for real people's likeness. Files are made by generative models and saved in your MegaScenes library.

## Install
In Claude Code:
```
/plugin marketplace add megascenes-app/claude-plugins
/plugin install graduation-announcement@megascenes
```

## Connecting
- Graduation Announcements talks to the MegaScenes server at `https://megascenes.com/api/claude/graduation-announcement/mcp` (Streamable HTTP).
- Connecting opens MegaScenes: sign in, create an account, or continue without one and add your email when you pay (OAuth with PKCE). Then you come straight back to the chat.
- Each graduation announcement uses credits from your MegaScenes account and is priced before it runs; new accounts can start with a low-cost trial week.
- The graduation announcements show in a viewer in the chat where Claude supports interactive apps; in Claude Code you get the links.

## Tools
| Tool | What it does |
|---|---|
| `list_graduation_announcement_models` | Models for graduation announcements with settings and starting prices. Read-only. |
| `estimate_graduation_announcement_cost` | Exact price of one graduation announcement before it runs. Read-only. |
| `create_graduation_announcement` | Starts one graduation announcement in your account. Uses credits; needs sign-in. |
| `get_graduation_announcement` | Status and file links. Read-only. |
| `get_account` | Your plan and credit balance. Read-only. |

## Data and privacy
What you give for the graduation announcement (the details you give) goes to MegaScenes, stays in your MegaScenes account with the finished file, and is passed to the model provider that makes it. The plugin has no hooks or local scripts and sends nothing else.
