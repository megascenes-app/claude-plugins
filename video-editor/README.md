# Video Editor

Video editor: edit a video from your MegaScenes library with a prompt. Restyle it, swap objects or the background, reframe it to 9:16 or 1:1, extend it with new footage, or lip-sync it to a voice track.

## What it does
Pick a video from your MegaScenes library (upload one in the Studio, or make one with the Video plugin) and say what to change: turn it into a watercolor or anime look, replace the car with a bicycle, put the scene in the snow, reframe a landscape clip for reels, add five more seconds, or match the lips to a voice-over.

**How the files are made:** each edit is generated by a video model that MegaScenes runs through its model providers. Nothing is made until you say yes, and the price in credits comes first. Most edits are priced by the length of the source video.

Starts from a video in your MegaScenes library: upload one in the Studio, or make one first. Lip sync also takes an audio file from the library.

## Install
In Claude Code:
```
/plugin marketplace add megascenes-app/claude-plugins
/plugin install video-editor@megascenes
```

## Connecting
- Video Editor talks to the MegaScenes server at `https://megascenes.com/api/claude/video-editor/mcp` (Streamable HTTP).
- Connecting opens MegaScenes: sign in, create an account, or continue without one and add your email when you pay (OAuth with PKCE). Then you come straight back to the chat.
- Each edit uses credits from your MegaScenes account and is priced before it runs; new accounts can start with a low-cost trial week.
- Edits take one to a few minutes: Claude checks on progress, and the video plays in a viewer in the chat where Claude supports interactive apps (a link in Claude Code).

## Tools
| Tool | What it does |
|---|---|
| `list_video_edit_models` | Models for video edits (restyle, reframe, extend, lip sync) with settings and starting prices. Read-only. |
| `estimate_video_edit_cost` | Exact price of one edit for a video of a given length, before it runs. Read-only. |
| `create_video_edit` | Starts one edit in your account. Uses credits; needs sign-in. |
| `get_video_edit` | Status and file links. Read-only. |
| `list_my_media` | Your uploaded and generated videos, audio and images, with each video's length, to pick the one to edit. Read-only. |
| `get_account` | Your plan and credit balance. Read-only. |

## Example prompts
- "Turn my latest video into a hand-drawn watercolor animation"
- "Reframe the beach clip to 9:16 for reels"
- "Extend the drone shot by 5 seconds as the camera pulls back"

## Data and privacy
What you give for the edit (the video and any audio or image you pick from your library, and the change you describe) goes to MegaScenes, stays in your MegaScenes account with the finished file, and is passed to the model provider that makes it. The plugin has no hooks or local scripts and sends nothing else.
