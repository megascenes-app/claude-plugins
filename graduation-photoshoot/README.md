# Graduation Photoshoot

Create graduation photos of yourself from your own selfie in your MegaScenes library: the same face in a cap and gown, on a campus lawn, tossing the cap or holding a diploma.

## What it does
Pick a selfie or a portrait of yourself from your MegaScenes library and choose the scene, the gown and the style: on a campus lawn at golden hour, tossing the cap, holding a diploma by old stone steps, in a black, navy or maroon gown, as an editorial portrait, a candid shot, golden hour or black and white. Add a class year if you like. You get a picture that keeps your face, portrait, square or tall for stories. For your graduation announcement, posts and profile pictures. Claude tells you the price first and makes it in your MegaScenes account when you say yes.

**How the files are made:** each graduation photo is generated by an image model. Nothing is made until you say yes, and the price in credits comes first.

Limitations: only from a selfie or portrait of yourself, never of a famous person; no swimwear, underwear or sensual shots. School crests and logos are not drawn, and a class year is passed to the model as written, so check the lettering in the result. The photo must already be in your MegaScenes library; attachments from the chat are not passed to the plugin. Making a file needs a signed-in MegaScenes account and runs in it; nothing is made until you confirm. Not for sexual or adult content. Files are made by generative models and saved in your MegaScenes library.

## Install
In Claude Code:
```
/plugin marketplace add megascenes-app/claude-plugins
/plugin install graduation-photoshoot@megascenes
```

## Connecting
- Graduation Photoshoot talks to the MegaScenes server at `https://megascenes.com/api/claude/graduation-photoshoot/mcp` (Streamable HTTP).
- Connecting opens MegaScenes: sign in, create an account, or continue without one and add your email when you pay (OAuth with PKCE). Then you come straight back to the chat.
- Each graduation photo uses credits from your MegaScenes account and is priced before it runs; new accounts can start with a low-cost trial week.
- The graduation photos show in a viewer in the chat where Claude supports interactive apps; in Claude Code you get the links.

## Tools
| Tool | What it does |
|---|---|
| `list_graduation_photoshoot_models` | Models for graduation photos with settings and starting prices. Read-only. |
| `estimate_graduation_photoshoot_cost` | Exact price of one graduation photo before it runs. Read-only. |
| `create_graduation_photoshoot` | Starts one graduation photo in your account. Uses credits; needs sign-in. |
| `get_graduation_photoshoot` | Status and file links. Read-only. |
| `list_my_images` | Your uploads and images made here, to pick the one to start from. Read-only. |
| `get_account` | Your plan and credit balance. Read-only. |

## Example prompts
- "Make a graduation photo of me in a cap and gown from my latest MegaScenes selfie"
- "Put me on a campus lawn at golden hour tossing my cap"
- "Create a black and white photo of me holding a diploma by old stone steps"

## Data and privacy
What you give for the graduation photo (the picture you pick from your library and the details you give) goes to MegaScenes, stays in your MegaScenes account with the finished file, and is passed to the model provider that makes it. The plugin has no hooks or local scripts and sends nothing else.
