# Easter Card Maker

Design Easter cards with your greeting, your message and your signature in the design, religious or not, in the look you choose.

## What it does
Write the main line, who the card is for, a short message and how you sign it, in your own words, religious or not, and pick the look: pastel spring flowers, painted eggs, cute bunnies, white lilies and a cross, watercolor. You get an Easter card with your words set in the design and its symbols drawn respectfully, portrait, square or wide, ready to print, post or send. Give only the words you want printed on the card: no addresses or contact details are needed. For family, friends, neighbors and everyone you send spring wishes to. Claude tells you the price first and makes it in your MegaScenes account when you say yes.

**How the files are made:** each Easter card is generated by an image model. Nothing is made until you say yes, and the price in credits comes first.

Limitations: designs as image files; your text is passed to the model as written, so check the lettering in the result. It does not mail the cards. Making a file needs a signed-in MegaScenes account and runs in it; nothing is made until you confirm. Not for sexual or adult content or for real people's likeness. Files are made by generative models and saved in your MegaScenes library.

## Install
In Claude Code:
```
/plugin marketplace add megascenes-app/claude-plugins
/plugin install easter-card@megascenes
```

## Connecting
- Easter Card Maker talks to the MegaScenes server at `https://megascenes.com/api/claude/easter-card/mcp` (Streamable HTTP).
- Connecting opens MegaScenes: sign in, create an account, or continue without one and add your email when you pay (OAuth with PKCE). Then you come straight back to the chat.
- Each Easter card uses credits from your MegaScenes account and is priced before it runs; new accounts can start with a low-cost trial week.
- The Easter cards show in a viewer in the chat where Claude supports interactive apps; in Claude Code you get the links.

## Tools
| Tool | What it does |
|---|---|
| `list_easter_card_models` | Models for Easter cards with settings and starting prices. Read-only. |
| `estimate_easter_card_cost` | Exact price of one Easter card before it runs. Read-only. |
| `create_easter_card` | Starts one Easter card in your account. Uses credits; needs sign-in. |
| `get_easter_card` | Status and file links. Read-only. |
| `get_account` | Your plan and credit balance. Read-only. |

## Example prompts

- "A cute bunny Easter card for Nonna: Happy Easter 2027"
- "An Easter card with white lilies and a cross and the words He is risen"

## Data and privacy
What you give for the Easter card (the details you give) goes to MegaScenes, stays in your MegaScenes account with the finished file, and is passed to the model provider that makes it. The plugin has no hooks or local scripts and sends nothing else.
