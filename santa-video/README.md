# Christmas Video from Santa

Create a short video of Santa saying a message you write, with a first name, for parents preparing a Christmas surprise.

## What it does
Write what Santa should say and the first name to use: a thank you for a good year, a wish for the holidays, a hint about a surprise. You get a short video of a classic Santa in his workshop saying your message, ready to show on Christmas morning. Give only the first name and the words Santa should say: no address, age or other personal details are needed. For parents and families preparing a Christmas surprise. Claude tells you the price first and makes it in your MegaScenes account when you say yes.

**How the files are made:** each clip is generated by a video model. Nothing is made until you say yes, and the price in credits comes first.

Limitations: a short clip of a few seconds; Santa is a classic, generic figure, not a real person or a character owned by a brand. Speech follows your words as closely as the model can. Making a file needs a signed-in MegaScenes account and runs in it; nothing is made until you confirm. Not for sexual or adult content or for real people's likeness. Files are made by generative models and saved in your MegaScenes library.

## Install
In Claude Code:
```
/plugin marketplace add megascenes-app/claude-plugins
/plugin install santa-video@megascenes
```

## Connecting
- Christmas Video from Santa talks to the MegaScenes server at `https://megascenes.com/api/claude/santa-video/mcp` (Streamable HTTP).
- Connecting opens MegaScenes: sign in, create an account, or continue without one and add your email when you pay (OAuth with PKCE). Then you come straight back to the chat.
- Each clip uses credits from your MegaScenes account and is priced before it runs; new accounts can start with a low-cost trial week.
- Clips take one to a few minutes: Claude checks on progress, and the clip plays in a viewer in the chat where Claude supports interactive apps (a link in Claude Code).

## Tools
| Tool | What it does |
|---|---|
| `list_santa_video_models` | Models for Santa videos with settings and starting prices. Read-only. |
| `estimate_santa_video_cost` | Exact price of one Santa video before it runs. Read-only. |
| `create_santa_video` | Starts one Santa video in your account. Uses credits; needs sign-in. |
| `get_santa_video` | Status and file links. Read-only. |
| `get_account` | Your plan and credit balance. Read-only. |

## Example prompts
- "A video of Santa saying: Merry Christmas Leo, I saw how much you helped at home this year"
- "Santa in his workshop telling Mia that a surprise is on its way"
- "A short video of Santa wishing the Rossi family happy holidays"

## Data and privacy
What you give for the clip (the details you give) goes to MegaScenes, stays in your MegaScenes account with the finished file, and is passed to the model provider that makes it. The plugin has no hooks or local scripts and sends nothing else.
