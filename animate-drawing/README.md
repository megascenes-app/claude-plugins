# Animate a Drawing

Bring a drawing to life: a photo or scan of a hand-drawn picture in your MegaScenes library, turned into a short animation that keeps the same lines, colors and paper.

## What it does
Pick a photo or a scan of the drawing from your MegaScenes library, a crayon dragon, a pencil sketch or your little one's artwork from the fridge, and describe how it should move: the dragon flaps its wings and flies, the sun smiles and the flowers sway, the rocket takes off. You get a short animation that keeps the same lines, colors and paper, as if the drawing itself came to life. For parents, grandparents and teachers who want to surprise the artist, and for anyone with a sketchbook. Claude tells you the price first and makes it in your MegaScenes account when you say yes.

**How the files are made:** each clip is generated by a video model. Nothing is made until you say yes, and the price in credits comes first.

Limitations: a short clip of a few seconds from one drawing, silent unless you ask for sound and the model offers it. The drawing must already be in your MegaScenes library; attachments from the chat are not passed to the plugin. Original drawings only, not characters or brands owned by others. Making a file needs a signed-in MegaScenes account and runs in it; nothing is made until you confirm. Not for sexual or adult content or for real people's likeness. Files are made by generative models and saved in your MegaScenes library.

## Install
In Claude Code:
```
/plugin marketplace add megascenes-app/claude-plugins
/plugin install animate-drawing@megascenes
```

## Connecting
- Animate a Drawing talks to the MegaScenes server at `https://megascenes.com/api/claude/animate-drawing/mcp` (Streamable HTTP).
- Connecting opens MegaScenes: sign in, create an account, or continue without one and add your email when you pay (OAuth with PKCE). Then you come straight back to the chat.
- Each clip uses credits from your MegaScenes account and is priced before it runs; new accounts can start with a low-cost trial week.
- Clips take one to a few minutes: Claude checks on progress, and the clip plays in a viewer in the chat where Claude supports interactive apps (a link in Claude Code).

## Tools
| Tool | What it does |
|---|---|
| `list_animated_drawing_models` | Models for animated drawings with settings and starting prices. Read-only. |
| `estimate_animated_drawing_cost` | Exact price of one animated drawing before it runs. Read-only. |
| `create_animated_drawing` | Starts one animated drawing in your account. Uses credits; needs sign-in. |
| `get_animated_drawing` | Status and file links. Read-only. |
| `list_my_images` | Your uploads and images made here, to pick the one to start from. Read-only. |
| `get_account` | Your plan and credit balance. Read-only. |

## Example prompts
- "Animate the dragon drawing in my MegaScenes library so it flaps its wings and flies"
- "Make the sun in my latest drawing smile and the flowers sway"
- "Bring my little one's rocket drawing to life as it takes off"

## Data and privacy
What you give for the clip (the picture you pick from your library and the details you give) goes to MegaScenes, stays in your MegaScenes account with the finished file, and is passed to the model provider that makes it. The plugin has no hooks or local scripts and sends nothing else.
