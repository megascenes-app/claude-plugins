# Graduation Invitations

Design graduation party invitations with the graduate's name, the class or degree, the date, the time and the place on them, in the colors you choose.

## What it does
Give the graduate's name, the class or degree and the party details you want printed, and pick the look: black and gold, navy and white, modern minimal, confetti and caps. You get an invitation with your words set in the design, portrait to print, tall to send from your phone, or square. Give only the words you want printed on the invitation: no guest list or contact details are needed. For graduates and families getting ready for a graduation party, from high school to a master's degree. Claude tells you the price first and makes it in your MegaScenes account when you say yes.

**How the files are made:** each graduation invitation is generated by an image model. Nothing is made until you say yes, and the price in credits comes first.

Limitations: designs as image files; your text is passed to the model as written, so check the lettering in the result. School crests and logos are not drawn. It does not send the invitations, keep guest lists or track replies. Making a file needs a signed-in MegaScenes account and runs in it; nothing is made until you confirm. Not for sexual or adult content or for real people's likeness. Files are made by generative models and saved in your MegaScenes library.

## Install
In Claude Code:
```
/plugin marketplace add megascenes-app/claude-plugins
/plugin install graduation-invitation@megascenes
```

## Connecting
- Graduation Invitations talks to the MegaScenes server at `https://megascenes.com/api/claude/graduation-invitation/mcp` (Streamable HTTP).
- Connecting opens MegaScenes: sign in, create an account, or continue without one and add your email when you pay (OAuth with PKCE). Then you come straight back to the chat.
- Each graduation invitation uses credits from your MegaScenes account and is priced before it runs; new accounts can start with a low-cost trial week.
- The graduation invitations show in a viewer in the chat where Claude supports interactive apps; in Claude Code you get the links.

## Tools
| Tool | What it does |
|---|---|
| `list_graduation_invitation_models` | Models for graduation invitations with settings and starting prices. Read-only. |
| `estimate_graduation_invitation_cost` | Exact price of one graduation invitation before it runs. Read-only. |
| `create_graduation_invitation` | Starts one graduation invitation in your account. Uses credits; needs sign-in. |
| `get_graduation_invitation` | Status and file links. Read-only. |
| `get_account` | Your plan and credit balance. Read-only. |

## Data and privacy
What you give for the graduation invitation (the details you give) goes to MegaScenes, stays in your MegaScenes account with the finished file, and is passed to the model provider that makes it. The plugin has no hooks or local scripts and sends nothing else.
